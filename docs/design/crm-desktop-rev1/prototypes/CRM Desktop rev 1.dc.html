TRY · RIGHT MENU, THEN THE PAGE

Pick from the right, work in the middle

Same pattern as Start a quote in the Revenue app. Opening Assignment, Settings or RS Profile slides a menu in from the right (520 px, 440 at 1280) beside the last page used, marked Current. Picking a row opens that page across the full CRM area and closes the menu; the rust chip next to the title (“All settings”) brings it back. Esc or × also closes it.

CRM DESKTOP REV 1 · STEP 1

The shared frame

Head, side pane, alerts and toast, used by the Control panel and the RS desktop. Pages are placeholders here; they come in step 2. Every frame is live: click tabs, Team | Mine, “Open…”, CRM alerts, close (or Esc).

Left bar · CRM inside Profile, collapsible The Revenue app nav stays as it is. The CRM lives where it does today, under Profile › My leads & appointments: Profile unfolds in the sidebar and the CRM pages sit indented below it with their rust counts, Team | Mine on top for the Director and leaders. Profile folds; the bar collapses to a 72 px icon rail (counts become corner badges), which gives the CRM area +168 px. The head keeps one 56 px row: page name, page controls, CRM alerts.
Side pane · three modes Docked 440 px (400 at 1280), always there on Inbox and Contacts, empty state when nothing is picked. Wide = right of a 380 px list on Assignment, Settings, RS Profile. Overlay 440 px over the page on Insight and Calendar, so the page never reflows (fixes the narrow month grid). Every pane gets a desktop header with title, context and close; the phone’s back link is never shown.
Two bells · made distinct The Revenue app bell stays in the top bar (memos, results, requests). The CRM one becomes a labelled “CRM alerts” button with a chat icon and count (Move, Share, Won, Lost, Overdue 9 h+); it always opens as an overlay pane. Toast: bottom centre of the CRM area, 3 s, Undo for Send to (30 s).
