## 1. Purpose

This annexure sets out the commission structure, qualification criteria, career
progression pathway, and incentive framework applicable to the Revenue Team —
Investment Renovation. It is intended to help members clearly understand how
they progress, how they qualify at each level, what they personally earn, what
their upline earns, how group sales and group expansion work, and what
additional incentives may be available.

This structure is separate and distinct from the Sales Incentive Programme
applicable to the Revenue Team (Fair). The two programmes should not be
cross-applied.

## 2. Key Definitions

The following terms are used throughout this annexure and must not be used
interchangeably:

- **Personal Sales** — sales generated directly by the employee.
- **Group Sales** — combined qualifying sales generated within the employee's
  applicable group/team structure.
- **Direct Line** — an employee directly positioned under the relevant leader
  within the Revenue Team hierarchy (not any employee positioned elsewhere in
  the structure).
- **Direct Commission / Override** — commission earned by the higher-positioned
  (upline) employee, based on the qualifying performance of the employee
  directly below them.
- **Personal Owned Achievement** — the monetary incentive earned by an employee
  from their own personal performance/achievement.
- **Group Incentive** — the monetary incentive earned when an employee's
  qualifying group reaches its required group-sales target.
- **House Assignment** — where the owner/Company assigns a house to a Revenue
  Specialist to oversee/manage the renovation project, regardless of tier
  will receive RM10 per month for as long as the agreement date.

## 3. Career Progression & Commission Structure

The hierarchy progresses as follows, based on increasing personal/group
performance and leadership responsibility:

Revenue Specialist → Team Assistant → Team Leader → Senior Team Leader

### 3.1 Revenue Specialist

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Commission | 3% |
| Individual KPI | RM100,000 in personal (individual) sales |

Current titles have no direct line recruit access. The RM100,000 KPI refers to
the employee's own personal sales, not group sales.

### 3.2 Team Assistant

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Commission | 3.5% |
| Qualification | RM200,000 in personal sales, achieved for 2 consecutive months |
| Override | Applicable 0.5% override over the previous employee level, subject to Company commission rules |
| Direct Line Limit | Maximum of 3 Direct Lines |

Qualification is based on the employee's own personal sales, not group sales.
This level recognises an employee who has demonstrated sufficient personal
sales performance to begin developing/supporting a team.

### 3.3 Team Leader

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Commission | 4% |
| Qualification | RM800,000 group sales |
| Direct Line Limit | Maximum of 3 Direct Lines |

A Direct Line refers to an employee directly positioned under the Team Leader
within the hierarchy — not any 3 employees at large. The structure must not
exceed the permitted 3 Direct Lines, in order to maintain a controlled team
structure.

### 3.4 Senior Team Leader

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Commission | 4% (no increase from Team Leader) |
| Qualification | RM1,000,000 group sales |
| Direct Line Limit | Maximum of 3 Direct Lines (if three direct lines achieved group sales, entitled additional 1 (ONE) direct line prior hitting achieved group sales of RM1,000,000) |

## 4. Senior Team Leader — Group Structure & Expansion

A Senior Team Leader may develop/manage up to 3 groups initially. Each group
must independently achieve RM1,000,000 in group sales:

| Group | Required Group Sales |
|---|---|
| Group 1 | RM1,000,000 |
| Group 2 | RM1,000,000 |
| Group 3 | RM1,000,000 |
| Total (3 groups) | RM3,000,000 |

Each group must independently achieve RM1,000,000 — the RM3,000,000 total
cannot be met by combining group results. For example, Group 1 = RM1M, Group 2
= RM1M, Group 3 = RM500K does NOT qualify the Senior Team Leader to open
another group; all 3 existing groups must individually reach RM1M.

Personal sales does count as a group effort: if the personal sales is only
RM200,000 but the group effect is RM800,000 in total, merging together as one
total of RM1,000,000 achieves the group sales.

A Senior Team Leader may only open an additional (4th+) group after all 3
existing groups have individually achieved their RM1,000,000 group-sales
target. If any existing group has not reached RM1M, no additional group may be
opened. This ensures existing groups are productive and properly developed
before further expansion.

## 5. Group Incentive

For every qualifying group that achieves RM1,000,000 in group sales, the Senior
Team Leader may receive an RM6,000 group incentive:

| Group | Achievement | Incentive |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | RM1,000,000 | RM6,000 |
| Group 2 | RM1,000,000 | RM6,000 |
| Group 3 | RM1,000,000 | RM6,000 |
| Total | RM3,000,000 | RM18,000 |

This incentive is based on qualifying group achievement and is separate from
the basic 4% commission.

## 6. Senior Team Leader Incentive & Progressive Car/Property Subsidy

The Senior Team Leader incentive structure is: RM6,000 Group Incentive +
RM2,000 Criteria-Based Incentive (per qualifying group). The RM2,000 component
is a car subsidy, subject to the following rules:

- The subsidy is NOT cash and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
- It is subject to the applicable Company criteria.
- It follows the applicable car loan amount/arrangement.
- It is claimable monthly, subject to the relevant group achievement and
  Company criteria.
- It should not be automatically treated as a cash allowance.

Progressive Subsidy Schedule

| Qualifying Group | Subsidy |
|---|---|
| First Group | RM2,000 |
| Second Group | RM2,000 + RM2,000 = RM4,000 |
| Third Group | RM2,000 + RM2,000 + RM2,000 = RM6,000 |

## 7. Direct Commission / Override

Direct Commission is NOT the same as Personal Owned Achievement (see Section
8).

- Direct Commission refers to the commission/override earned by the
  higher-positioned employee (upline), based on the qualifying performance of
  the employee directly below them, in recognition of developing/managing that
  person.
- If there is no qualifying person above the employee, no Direct Line/Override
  is payable.
- The override applies only to the previous qualifying employee level /
  immediate higher position in the approved hierarchy — this is not unlimited
  commission from every employee underneath the structure.

## 8. Personal Owned Achievement

Personal Owned Achievement is the monetary incentive earned by an employee from
their own personal performance/achievement — completely distinct from Direct
Commission.

| Achievement Type | Who Earns It |
|---|---|
| Personal achievement (employee's own sales) | Personal Owned Achievement — earned by the employee themselves |
| Achievement generated by the person below (in the hierarchy) | Direct Line/Override — earned by the applicable upline, if hierarchy and conditions are satisfied |

## 9. Introduction & Closing

Where an employee introduces a potential customer/lead and the opportunity is
subsequently successfully closed, the introducing employee may qualify for the
applicable Closing percentage, subject to the Company's qualification rules.
Introduction of a lead and successful closing are treated as separate
components — an employee may introduce a customer even if they are not the
person who ultimately completes the closing. Where the applicable rules are
satisfied, the introducing employee may receive the relevant closing
percentage.

## 10. Compulsory KPI Requirements & Tier Structure

There are three compulsory achievement components that must be met, according
to the applicable tier/commission structure, to qualify for the relevant
monetary additional incentive:

- Appointment
- Closing
- Revenue

If an employee fails to achieve any one of the three compulsory requirements,
the applicable monetary incentive is automatically forfeited for that period.
Partial achievement does not automatically entitle the employee to the monetary
incentive.

Tier Structure

| Component | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appointment | 20% | 25% | 30% |
| Closing | 30% | 40% | 40% |
| Revenue Target | RM200,000 | RM300,000 | RM400,000 |
| Direct Line | RM300 | RM500 | RM800 |
| Personal Owned Achievement | RM800 | RM2,000 | RM4,500 |

These percentages/amounts represent achievement, commission, and incentive
criteria for the applicable tier. They should not be automatically interpreted
as a percentage of revenue unless explicitly stated by management.

## 11. Advertisement Control

Advertising and lead-generation activities must be controlled and coordinated
with the Marketing Team, including:

- Advertisement planning and campaign planning
- Advertisement content
- Lead generation and lead allocation
- Advertising budget
- Campaign performance and promotional activities

Revenue Team members should not independently conduct advertising activities
outside the Company's approved process.

## 12. Hierarchy Summary

| Level | Commission | Qualification | Key Incentive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue Specialist | 3% | RM100K individual KPI | - |
| Team Assistant | 3.5% | RM200K personal sales × 2 consecutive months | 0.5% override (applicable) |
| Team Leader | 4% | RM800K group sales + 3 Direct Lines | 0.5% override + 0.5% override (applicable) |
| Senior Team Leader | 4% | RM1M group sales; up to 3 qualifying groups | RM6,000/group + RM2,000 progressive subsidy/group |

IMPORTANT NOTICE: This Investment Renovation commission and career progression
structure applies to the Revenue Team — Investment Renovation only and is
separate from the Sales Incentive Programme applicable to the Revenue Team
(Fair). Management reserves the right to review and amend this structure,
including commission percentages, qualification criteria, and incentive
arrangements, from time to time depending on business needs.
