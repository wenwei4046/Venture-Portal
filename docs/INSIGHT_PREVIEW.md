# Insight mobile preview (2026-09-13)

The Arena companion section replaces the preview's Income navigation entry with Insight. It reuses the Arena Espresso/Clay and Cocoa/Apricot tokens. Entry: `/member/preview?design=compare&view=insight`; direct phone view: `/member/preview?design=a&view=insight`.

- Personal and Team are the two overview perspectives. Personal contains sales against a monthly target, weighted margin, KPI item count and a venue entry. Team contains downline progress and a fair selector. Order lists, order details, venue lists, fair standings, targets and KPI products are separate screens with URL-backed navigation and filters.
- A fair is identified by venue, organizer and dated occurrence. Mid Valley's MLE Modern Home Living and Big Home results are intentionally distinct. Fair standings rank every participant by sales, including peers outside the downline; only the current member and downlines open into performance details. Ties share a place.
- Targets always use whole-month, all-venue progress, including while viewing a week or a single fair. Blended margin weights only orders with known margins by their sales amounts. Missing values remain unavailable. Orders show category, order number, sales amount, margin tier, KPI quantity and sample commission amount/status.
- A low-margin order can open an order-exclusion comparison for a sales meeting. The example Marcus order is RM24,000 at 18% margin, 60% of last week's RM40,000 sales; removing it changes the weighted margin from 26.6% to 39.5%. This is an explanatory comparison, not a forecast or an instruction to cancel an order.
- This is a design preview with deterministic sample data. Actual Insight already has personal and team aggregates, but does not yet return downline order details, fair occurrence/organizer, category or order-linked commission. A future live endpoint must use server-side subtree authorization, paginated order queries and independent aggregate totals; the existing 200-order snapshot must not be used as the whole-period denominator.
- Categories in these fixtures are single-category orders. Live multi-category/package orders need explicit category tags or a primary-category rule so revenue is not counted twice. Actual tiers are brand-configured (Tier 2 can be above Tier 1); monthly incentives must remain separate from per-bill commission. A settled commission is not proof of bank payment. The sample `paid` state does not map automatically from an applied payroll run.

Validation: focused arithmetic and interaction tests, TypeScript and ESLint checks, plus phone layout/interaction inspection at 320, 390 and 430 px. Changes live in the `codex/insight-mobile` worktree, based on the existing uncommitted Arena preview draft.

KPI refinement: the KPI screen now explains order coverage before opening the list: orders with at least one qualifying item / all selected orders, the count without KPI, total items, average KPI items per order, and average sales amount per order. Both overview averages include orders without KPI; an empty denominator displays unavailable. Only two recent qualifying order numbers are previewed before a prominent View sales orders action; the full order list remains a separate screen. Monthly target is a secondary row, with product breakdown collapsed.

The KPI order route uses `lens=kpi` and an exact `quantity` filter. `any` means at least one KPI item; plus/minus selects 0, 1, 2, 3 and larger available quantities, with 0 explicitly meaning orders without KPI. Category, search, fair, person and period filters combine with quantity and persist through order details/back/reload. The list average uses matching orders only and is labeled Avg / match. Quantity replaces the tier dropdown only in this KPI view; the margin entry retains its tier filters. Jia's six illustrative orders now have 3, 0, 2, 6, 1 and 0 KPI items: 12 items, 4/6 coverage (66.7%), 2 items per order. Financial figures and the Marcus margin example are unchanged.

Visual hierarchy: a solid olive coverage card leads the KPI view, followed by compact averages, a white order-preview panel and a solid primary action. Insight's sales card remains the dominant espresso block; secondary metrics use white surfaces with clay/olive accents, and the margin diagnosis has a separate clay surface. Existing venue/ranking navigation is retained.

September refinement: the period picker now has its own ivory surface, visible espresso border and focus ring. Sales targets → Explore this month’s orders opens a third `lens=sales` listing. This combined view supports category, margin tier, exact KPI quantity (+/−), search and venue together. Any quantity includes zero-KPI orders; the dedicated KPI lens still defaults to qualifying orders, and the margin lens retains its simpler tier filters. Selections persist through order details and browser navigation. Venue keeps a full-width selector and selected organizer/date context to distinguish fairs at the same location.

Monthly quick insights: a comment icon on personal/downline performance opens a floating summary; Team has its own summary button. The fixed September 1–13 preview is compared with fictional August 1–13 order facts, across all venues, independently of the background filters. Reports use weighted margin, sales growth, KPI change/coverage and monthly targets. Team covers the three direct downlines, excluding self and venue-ranking peers. Missing costing is excluded from margin, and missing history is distinct from a known zero baseline. The initial window shows a short calculated conclusion, comparison metrics and linked low-margin evidence; team-member comparisons and calculation details expand on demand. Native dialog focus containment, Escape/backdrop close, scroll isolation and trigger-focus restoration support mobile and keyboard use. Evidence links reset the date/venue context to the report scope and open the correct member/order.

These summaries and sales-meeting notes follow deterministic data rules; there is no AI service or live ERP connection. A Closer Look requires a calculable downward margin impact and a low-tier order in the view. An order's sales-meeting panel requires a known low-tier margin and a costed comparison after exclusion. Recommendations distinguish orders dragging the blend down from low-tier orders above the seller's blend, whose removal would actually lower it. The UI labels all figures as illustrative sample data.

## Independent integration

Insight owns its review surface, shell, and scoped --insight-* theme tokens. Its preview routes are selected only when view starts with insight. No Arena components, scoring rules, ranking interfaces, global theme edits, or changes to the live member workspace are included. The existing Arena navigation link is retained as the destination contract for that separately developed section. Personal and Team performance here remain illustrative preview data; no live API or AI service was added.
