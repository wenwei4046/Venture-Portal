## Where colours and fonts are defined today

- **Revenue app tokens** (`--rv-*`, used by the whole Revenue app, not only the CRM): `src/app/globals.css` lines ~170–310. For example `--rv-ink #3F2B22`, `--rv-sub #806B60`, `--rv-paper-warm #FBF7F3`.
- **The CRM's own colours are inline hex in the generated screens**, as each prototype wrote them. There are 222 distinct values across `sales/`, `panel/`, `desk/` and `shared/`. `PanelApp.tsx` has 628 uses and `DeskApp.tsx` has 1,430.
- Constants: `desk/vm/tokens.ts` (desktop colours, team colours `teamColours`, `DIRECTOR_C`), `shared/chat-media.ts` (`MINE_BG`, `MINE_TIME`, `tickColor`), and the status colours in each `vm/` (e.g. New `#7A4FB0`).
- `crm.module.css` and `desk/*.module.css` set the resets and a few states.
- **Fonts**: Inter is the CRM's `.app` body. Inter Tight comes from `--font-display-rv` (`src/app/layout.tsx`, next/font). Phone sizes use `calc(Npx * var(--rv-type, 1))` (`type-scale.cjs`, `shared/type.ts` › `typePx`); `--rv-type` is 0.92 on a phone and 1 on the desktop.

## Plan (for your OK)

- **One token file** for the CRM: `src/components/member/crm/crm-tokens.css`. It holds the README's tokens as `--crm-*` on the CRM's roots (`.app`, the desk root), so the rest of the Revenue app does not change.
- **Each generator gets a palette pass** (`tools/crm-design/palette-4a.cjs`), run before `type-scale.cjs`. It turns every old hex into its token and **refuses an unmapped colour**, so no raw hex is left in a screen.
- **Layout follows the 4a frames.** Each frame's screen is reworked through its generator's patches (or regenerated from the 4a markup where the change is large), keeping every live-data binding and every behaviour the frames don't draw.
- **Check:** each screen is captured at the frame's size, phone 430 × 932 (`capture-redesign.mjs`) and desktop 1440 × 900 / 1920 × 1080. It is compared side by side with `capture-4a.mjs`'s frame. Motion (sheet slide, swipe, panel slide, hover / press) is checked against the prototype's transitions.
