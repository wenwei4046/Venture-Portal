# Marketing tab — Marketing's own view

Marketing's desktop view, `/marketing/**` — `src/app/(portal)/marketing/`, `src/components/mkt/`, `src/lib/marketing/`, `src/lib/marketing-view.ts`; migs `20261023214000_marketing_permission.sql` (the permission, the templates, the tabs) `20261023214100_marketing_core.sql` (the tables and readers) and `20261023234000_marketing_tasks.sql` (quick and recurring tasks: the writes, the task page's reader). The visual spec is the Claude Design handoff "HOUZS Marketing Module Setup", copied to `.design/marketing/design_handoff_houzs_marketing/` (README = spec, each `design/*.dc.html` = a screen and its logic, `hm-store.js` = the stage machine); `docs/module-spec-marketing.md` holds the build plan and the gaps against the portal. How a page and its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

**THE VIEW (2026-10-23 — the user: "这个东西是当做 Marketing Department 的 marketing view（一个单独的 department view）… 这里其实就是他们的 desktop view").** Marketing's own shell (`components/mkt/shell`, `.mkt-shell` tokens in `globals.css`): the handoff's sidebar (248px, folds to 64 — cookie `vp-mkt-nav`; My Space, Calendar, Subsidiaries with their counts, Projects, MANAGER TOOLS, at the foot My claims, Meeting Rooms and Settings), the white top bar (crumb, search ⌘K, Me and memos for a personal login, the bell), the page on a white card. Settings opens Appearance — font size S/M/L/XL = 12/13/14.5/16px, Inter / Nunito / IBM Plex Sans, Compact / Comfortable — kept in cookie `vp-mkt-look`, so the server draws it as chosen. Marketing's own pages draw on the card; the portal pages it keeps (`/claims`, `/admin/rooms`, Change password, IT support) on HR's white sheet.
- **Who wears it** (`isMarketingView`): the Principal's Marketing lens (`vp-view-as` = `marketing`, a TS-only lens role — lib/view-as.ts) and an account of the staff kind whose tabs hold `mkt.overview.view`. Decided by tabs, never by a kind of its own (permissions.md › THE DEPT MANAGER IS GONE). HR's and Finance's accounts keep theirs. `/dashboard` sends it to `/marketing`.
- **A phone keeps the portal's phone shell** — the user: "cause last once desktop view done, i will do marketing app mobile". No phone punching from Marketing until that app (Me on the desktop top bar punches from a computer).
- **No descriptions on the page** (DESIGN.md): the prototype's subtitles, ledes and hints ("Changes apply instantly", "What each person is on right now…", the stat cards' hints) were left out; empty states and live data stay.

**THE PERMISSION (mig 20261023214000).** Function `marketing` (Departments): View (the view's pages), Create (start a project / Quick / Recurring task), Edit (work the stages one is on), Approve (decide a stage as its approver), Manage (the manager tools: Tracking, the builders, plans, Replace Person, clients, cost and margin) — every reach `all`. Key `marketing`, scope none (no row policy opens on it). Held by the Principal login (role default), Ultimate and Full, and two new templates:
- **Marketing · Manager** — every cell; the user: managers = **MM + AMM** (titles MARKETING MANAGER, ASSISTANT MARKETING MANAGER).
- **Marketing · Staff** — View and Edit; Marketing's other titles (those that were on Staff · Basic).
- Both carry what Staff · Basic gives (the Overview, one's own claims, the rooms), and their **ticks are stored, Marketing's view only**: worked out from cells, Staff · Basic's claims and rooms tick the Department view's tabs beside Marketing's (one reach, side by side — `tabsFromCells`), and the template would live in two views. `permission-tabs.test.ts` holds the stored ticks to the cells (no loss, no gain).
- Tabs (`TabView` `mkt`, after `dept`; its tabs are listed BEFORE the Department view's in PERMISSION_TABS): `mkt.overview` My Space · `mkt.calendar` · `mkt.projects` (View/Create/Edit/Approve) · `mkt.tracking` (Marketing · Manage) · `mkt.my_claims` (opens on `marketing`, so Staff · Basic keeps the Department view's) · `mkt.meeting_rooms` (shared). The DB registry rows are added BY NAME (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) — production holds the Leads tabs main does not.
- `/marketing` asks `requirePermission('marketing')` after its tab (My Space's tab, like every Overview, asks no key); `/marketing/tracking` asks `requireActionOn('/marketing/tracking', 'marketing', 'manage')`.
- Marketing's history (`mkt_subsidiaries`, `mkt_team`, read key `marketing`) is read in Marketing's view, never the portal's Audit Log: `marketing` is an `IN_APP_HISTORY_KEYS` key, not an `AUDIT_READ_KEYS` one — Marketing · View is no reason to give its staff the Audit Log.

**THE DATA (mig 20261023214100).** Tables RLS on, NO policy, no client grants — every read and write is a SECURITY DEFINER function asking `has_any_action` (as Attendance's and IT's):
- `mkt_subsidiaries` — who a project is for: the group's companies (seeded once from active `companies`, codes CAR 2990 HC HK OHN DC CMI CONTS, the handoff's dots and emoji; the handoff's "Colive" is Ohana's place) **and Marketing's own clients outside the group** (no company — the user, on "+ Add subsidiary": "会，marketing 自己的客户名单"). `companies` is never written.
- `mkt_team` — Marketing's word over the roster: a person's team (manager / content / social / design / ads / brand), the words under their name, colour, capacity (load slots, default 5), in or out. The roster (`mkt_roster()`) is the Marketing department's active People, a row here only saying otherwise; a title's default team is `mkt_title_team()` (a guess the manager overrides — see the open questions).
- `mkt_projects` (Track ID `MKT-` product · `TSK-` quick · `RCR-` recurring, `-Rnn` a run; status minutes → waiting_pic → changes / approved → active → paused / done / cancelled), `mkt_stages` (the stage machine of `hm-store.js`: planned → todo → accepted → review (through `appr`, a person or the PIC, in turn) / changes → done), `mkt_subtasks` (a split stage's pieces, delivered back to its owner), `mkt_events` (each project's trail). A recurring task is a **series** row (its rule in `recur`, its stages the pattern) and a project per run; a series never counts as work (`isSeries`).
- Readers: `mkt_board(from, to)` (the roster, subsidiaries, projects open or of the last 60 days or with a stage due in the window ≤ 400 days, their stages and subtasks, HR's holidays), `mkt_shell()` (the sidebar's counts — what My Space draws, see below), `mkt_search(q)`, `mkt_notices(n)`; all on `READS_WHILE_VIEWING_AS` and `DEFINER_RPCS`. A notice is marked read with the portal's own `readNotices` (no Marketing writer). Notices: kind `marketing`, ref `mkt_project` → `/marketing/projects/<id>`.
- **Writes come with the pages that make them** (New Project, the task page, the builders — the PR plan in the module spec). Each is a DEFINER function asking the action its gate asks, the tab-write guard where a box narrows (none does yet: each Marketing cell has one box), and a line in `mkt_events`.

**MY SPACE, THE CALENDAR, PROJECTS, TRACKING (2026-10-23, PR ①).**
- **My Space's columns** (`lib/marketing/my-space.ts`): My turn — the viewer's stage to Accept (`todo`), in hand (`accepted`), sent back (`changes`, red), an approval waiting for them, their own subtask; Delegated by me — subtasks out with others (Ready → Review); Waiting on others — their stage in review (a person, or the PIC with the days waited), their delivery waiting for the owner. **Red = a date passed, or changes asked.** Red first, then the soonest date. The sidebar's My Space count (`mkt_shell`) is My turn plus the deliveries to Review — kept equal by `validate-marketing.mjs`. The room, the pet, the lobby, chat, Edit layout come last (the user's order ⑦).
- **The Calendar's layers**: ⏱ stage due dates (⚡ a Quick Task's, 🔁 a series' runs still to open); 📣 posts and 🗓️ shoots & meetings are drawn, empty, until their sources exist (the posting canvas, the minutes). It reads four months back and eight ahead (`readWindow`), and a month outside that is read again (`?d=`). `?who=` (the search's people) shows one person's dates.
- **Projects** (the handoff leaves the list undrawn): every project, Quick Task and run, Open / Done / All, by subsidiary (`?sub=`, the sidebar's rows) or Track ID / words (`?q=`, the search). `/marketing/projects/[id]` (an id or a Track ID) is the task page (PR ②a, below).
- **Tracking** (`lib/marketing/tracking.ts`): red (date passed; a delivery returned 3+ times) → waiting (an approval) → risk (due today; not accepted after a working day; the stages left need more working days than remain to the project's date) → ok. Working days are Monday–Friday less HR's holidays (`att_holidays`). The period (This week / This month / Custom) scopes the on-time % and days per stage — it never hides ongoing work. Nudge: below.

**QUICK AND RECURRING TASKS (2026-10-23, PR ②a — mig 20261023234000).**
- **New Project** (`/marketing/new`, the Projects tab; Marketing · Create; **+ New Project** in the top bar, never while viewing as somebody): a subsidiary, then 📦 Product project (**not built yet** — it needs ③'s Product, Workflow and Minutes builders, so ②b comes after ③), ⚡ Quick task or 🔁 Recurring task — a title, a type (Ads, Social Media, Content (Video), Brand, Other), what needs to be done (written; *Use meeting minutes* waits for the Minutes Builder), and its stages in the quick Workflow Builder: a name, a person from Marketing's roster (picking them sets the stage's team), 1–30 working days, up to three approval levels, where changes go back to, the fields it fills in (Text, Number, Select, Multi-select, Date, Person, File, Checkbox, Table with its columns, Note). Its *Canvas template* tab waits for ③.
- **Approvers are Marketing's managers** (roster team `manager` — the user: MM + AMM hold the manager's tools). The database answers permissions only for the caller, so the picker cannot ask who else holds Marketing · Approve; the decision itself asks Approve of whoever decides. Somebody else as an approver is a question for the user, not a guess.
- **Track IDs**: `TSK-` / `RCR-` / `MKT-` + the subsidiary's code + the office's YYMM + a number the three share for that subsidiary and month; a run adds `-Rnn` (to 999).
- **Dates**: the first stage starts the day the task does; each is due its SLA in working days after the one before (the first, after that day) — Monday to Friday less HR's holidays. `stageDates` (`lib/marketing/tasks.ts`) and `mkt_stage_dates` agree, both tested on the same days.
- **The stage machine** is `hm-store.js`'s: todo → Accept (its owner, Marketing · Edit) → the work, saved field by field (only what changed — a field cleared is sent as null) → Submit for approval (its approvers in turn, a version each time) or Complete stage (none), refused until every field is filled in (a stage with no field: a note or a link) → each approver Approves or Requests changes with a reason (Marketing · Approve, only the approver now). A stage whose owner starts the task opens accepted. Changes go back to the stage the builder named (it takes them; those between wait again), else stay. The last stage done closes the task.
- **Notices** (kind `marketing`, never to whoever acts; a phone that subscribed hears them too): *You're on quick / recurring task "…"* once per person (their first stage), *Your turn: <stage>*, *<owner> submitted <stage> vN for your approval*, *Changes requested on <stage>* (*— back to <stage>*), *<name> nudged you: <stage>*.
- **Nudge** — Marketing · Manage, on Tracking's person card (their task that waits on them most): a notice to whoever the task waits on (its owner, or its approver — never the PIC), at most once an hour per person and task, a line on the trail. The handoff's WhatsApp is not wired (phone numbers are People's — asked).
- **Recurring tasks**: the series keeps the rule (every day; every 1, 2 or 4 weeks on the weekdays chosen; every month on the 1st, the 15th or the last day; from a start not before today, until a day, after 1–60 runs, or no end) and every day it opens a run on (`recur.runs`); each run is a project made up to 60 days ahead that **opens on its day** — before it, the run is on no board and nobody's plate and cannot be accepted. Nothing runs on a clock: **every Marketing write tops the series up** (`mkt_top_up_due`). A series ends by itself after its last run; a manager pauses it (runs not yet open wait; one whose day passes meanwhile is skipped on resuming), resumes it, or ends it (runs not yet open are cancelled). A run already open carries on.
- **The task page** (`/marketing/projects/<id or Track ID>`, opened from My Space's tiles and the Calendar's dates): where it stands and with whom, the viewer's actions now, the stage rail, Requirements (locked), each stage's work so far (latest first), a recurring task's runs (Pause / Resume / End for a manager), the **Free space** (text, headings, checklists, dividers — anybody on the task, or a manager, block by block so two people never undo each other) and the trail in the store's words (the Free space's writes are kept in `mkt_events`, not shown). A **File** field holds a link until Marketing's R2 bucket. Every write answers with the task as it now stands — no router refresh.
- **My Space's Accept** accepts on the tile, then the task opens.

**FILES AND R2 (2026-10-23).** Another session is moving file storage to Cloudflare R2 (`feat/r2-storage`): `supabase.storage.from(bucket)` stays the API, authorisation moves to `public.file_objects` rows with their own policies. Marketing creates **no** Supabase Storage buckets or policies and touches no storage code; until R2's helpers are on main, Marketing's files are **links**. When they land: bucket `marketing`, keys `<uuid>/<name>`, Marketing's own INSERT / SELECT / DELETE policies on `file_objects` in a migration after theirs; the PIC page reads through the anon client with `x-link-code` and a DEFINER `marketing_link_may_read(name, code)`. The user: **≤ 50 MB uploaded, bigger files as links**.
