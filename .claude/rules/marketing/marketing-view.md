# Marketing tab — Marketing's own view

Marketing's desktop view, `/marketing/**` — `src/app/(portal)/marketing/`, `src/components/mkt/`, `src/lib/marketing/`, `src/lib/marketing-view.ts`; migs `20261023214000_marketing_permission.sql` (the permission, the templates, the tabs) `20261023214100_marketing_core.sql` (the tables and readers) `20261023234000_marketing_tasks.sql` (quick and recurring tasks: the writes, the task page's reader) `20261023235500_marketing_workflows.sql` (the Workflow Builder's templates and versions) `20261023235800_marketing_minutes.sql` (the Minutes Builder's) `20261023235900_marketing_products.sql` (the Product Builder's products and versions) `20261024001000_marketing_canvas.sql` (a workflow template's canvas) `20261024002000_marketing_product_projects.sql` (product projects and their minutes) `20261024003000_marketing_pic_link.sql` (the PIC's link) and `20261024004000_marketing_clients.sql` (Marketing's own clients). The visual spec is the Claude Design handoff "HOUZS Marketing Module Setup", copied to `.design/marketing/design_handoff_houzs_marketing/` (README = spec, each `design/*.dc.html` = a screen and its logic, `hm-store.js` = the stage machine); `docs/module-spec-marketing.md` holds the build plan and the gaps against the portal. How a page and its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

Settings opens Appearance — font size S/M/L/XL = 12/13/14.5/16px, Inter / Nunito / IBM Plex Sans, Compact / Comfortable — kept in cookie `vp-mkt-look`, so the server draws it as chosen. Marketing's own pages draw on the card; the portal pages it keeps (`/claims`, `/admin/rooms`, Change password, IT support) on HR's white sheet.
- **Who wears it** (`isMarketingView`): the Principal's Marketing lens (`vp-view-as` = `marketing`, a TS-only lens role — lib/view-as.ts) and an account of the staff kind whose tabs hold `mkt.overview.view`. Decided by tabs, never by a kind of its own (permissions.md › THE DEPT MANAGER IS GONE). HR's and Finance's accounts keep theirs. `/dashboard` sends it to `/marketing`.
- **A phone keeps the portal's phone shell** — the user: "cause last once desktop view done, i will do marketing app mobile". No phone punching from Marketing until that app (Me on the desktop top bar punches from a computer).
- **No descriptions on the page** (DESIGN.md): the prototype's subtitles, ledes and hints ("Changes apply instantly", "What each person is on right now…", the stat cards' hints) were left out; empty states and live data stay.

**MY SPACE, THE CALENDAR, PROJECTS, TRACKING (2026-10-23, PR ①).**
- **My Space's columns** (`lib/marketing/my-space.ts`): My turn — the viewer's stage to Accept (`todo`), in hand (`accepted`), sent back (`changes`, red), an approval waiting for them, their own subtask; Delegated by me — subtasks out with others (Ready → Review); Waiting on others — their stage in review (a person, or the PIC with the days waited), their delivery waiting for the owner. **Red = a date passed, or changes asked.** Red first, then the soonest date. The sidebar's My Space count (`mkt_shell`) is My turn plus the deliveries to Review — kept equal by `validate-marketing.mjs`. The room, the pet, the lobby, chat, Edit layout come last (the user's order ⑦).
- **The Calendar's layers**: ⏱ stage due dates (⚡ a Quick Task's, 🔁 a series' runs still to open); 📣 posts and 🗓️ shoots & meetings are drawn, empty, until their sources exist (the posting canvas, the minutes). It reads four months back and eight ahead (`readWindow`), and a month outside that is read again (`?d=`). `?who=` (the search's people) shows one person's dates.
- **Projects** (the handoff leaves the list undrawn): every project, Quick Task and run, Open / Done / All, by subsidiary (`?sub=`, the sidebar's rows) or Track ID / words (`?q=`, the search). `/marketing/projects/[id]` (an id or a Track ID) is the task page (PR ②a, below) — or a product project's own page (②b-1).
- **Tracking** (`lib/marketing/tracking.ts`): red (date passed; a delivery returned 3+ times) → waiting (an approval) → risk (due today; not accepted after a working day; the stages left need more working days than remain to the project's date) → ok. Working days are Monday–Friday less HR's holidays (`att_holidays`). The period (This week / This month / Custom) scopes the on-time % and days per stage — it never hides ongoing work. Nudge: below.

**QUICK AND RECURRING TASKS (2026-10-23, PR ②a — mig 20261023234000).**
- **New Project** (`/marketing/new`, the Projects tab; Marketing · Create; **+ New Project** in the top bar, never while viewing as somebody): a subsidiary, then 📦 Product project (the manager's — PRODUCT PROJECTS, below), ⚡ Quick task or 🔁 Recurring task — a title, a type (Ads, Social Media, Content (Video), Brand, Other), what needs to be done (written — or meeting minutes since ②a-3, below), and its stages in the quick Workflow Builder: a name, a person from Marketing's roster (picking them sets the stage's team), 1–30 working days, up to three approval levels, where changes go back to, the fields it fills in (Text, Number, Select, Multi-select, Date, Person, File, Checkbox, Table with its columns, Note). Its *Canvas template* tab waits for ③.
- **Approvers are Marketing's managers** (roster team `manager` — the user: MM + AMM hold the manager's tools). The database answers permissions only for the caller, so the picker cannot ask who else holds Marketing · Approve; the decision itself asks Approve of whoever decides. Somebody else as an approver is a question for the user, not a guess.
- **Track IDs**: `TSK-` / `RCR-` / `MKT-` + the subsidiary's code + the office's YYMM + a number the three share for that subsidiary and month; a run adds `-Rnn` (to 999).
- **Dates**: the first stage starts the day the task does; each is due its SLA in working days after the one before (the first, after that day) — Monday to Friday less HR's holidays. `stageDates` (`lib/marketing/tasks.ts`) and `mkt_stage_dates` agree, both tested on the same days.
- **The stage machine** is `hm-store.js`'s: todo → Accept (its owner, Marketing · Edit) → the work, saved field by field (only what changed — a field cleared is sent as null) → Submit for approval (its approvers in turn, a version each time) or Complete stage (none), refused until every field is filled in (a File field: a link or a file of its own; a stage with no field: a note, a file or a link — ②b-2e) → each approver Approves or Requests changes with a reason (Marketing · Approve, only the approver now). A stage whose owner starts the task opens accepted. Changes go back to the stage the builder named (it takes them; those between wait again), else stay. The last stage done closes the task.
- **Notices** (kind `marketing`, never to whoever acts; a phone that subscribed hears them too): *You're on quick / recurring task "…"* once per person (their first stage), *Your turn: <stage>*, *<owner> submitted <stage> vN for your approval*, *Changes requested on <stage>* (*— back to <stage>*), *<name> nudged you: <stage>*.
- **Nudge** — Marketing · Manage, on Tracking's person card (their task that waits on them most): a notice to whoever the task waits on (its owner, or its approver — never the PIC), at most once an hour per person and task, a line on the trail. The handoff's WhatsApp is not wired (phone numbers are People's — asked).
- **Recurring tasks**: the series keeps the rule (every day; every 1, 2 or 4 weeks on the weekdays chosen; every month on the 1st, the 15th or the last day; from a start not before today, until a day, after 1–60 runs, or no end) and every day it opens a run on (`recur.runs`); each run is a project made up to 60 days ahead that **opens on its day** — before it, the run is on no board and nobody's plate and cannot be accepted. Nothing runs on a clock: **every Marketing write tops the series up** (`mkt_top_up_due`). A series ends by itself after its last run; a manager pauses it (runs not yet open wait; one whose day passes meanwhile is skipped on resuming), resumes it, or ends it (runs not yet open are cancelled). A run already open carries on.
- **The task page** (`/marketing/projects/<id or Track ID>`, opened from My Space's tiles and the Calendar's dates): where it stands and with whom, the viewer's actions now, the stage rail, Requirements (locked), each stage's work so far (latest first), a recurring task's runs (Pause / Resume / End for a manager), the **Free space** (text, headings, checklists, dividers — anybody on the task, or a manager, block by block so two people never undo each other) and the trail in the store's words (the Free space's writes are kept in `mkt_events`, not shown). A **File** field holds a link until Marketing's R2 bucket. Every write answers with the task as it now stands — no router refresh.
- **My Space's Accept** accepts on the tile, then the task opens.

**THE WORKFLOW BUILDER (2026-10-23, PR ③a — mig 20261023235500).**
- **Where**: `/marketing/workflows`, MANAGER TOOLS › 🧩 Workflow Builder, Marketing · Manage. It joins the Tracking Dashboard's tab, relabelled **Manager tools** (`mkt.tracking`) — the builders, plans and Replace Person to come join it too: a second box asking `marketing.manage` would make the cell narrow, and then every DEFINER write asking it (Nudge, a series paused…) would have to ask its pages first (`tab-write-guards`).
- **A template** belongs to an **action** ("Reels video") and has its own name ("Full production", "Quick edit"); the two together are unique, whatever the case. Actions are not rows: the Actions column groups the templates by it, an action's emoji its first template's (✨ for a new one — the handoff has no emoji picker). **+ New action** makes it with a *Standard* template of one stage (named for the action, Design's, picked at assignment, 2 days, the PIC approves — the handoff's `mk()`); **+ Add template** adds *Template n*; **Clone** copies a template's draft into the same action as "… copy" (then "… copy 2") — never its versions. Nothing is seeded (the handoff's Reels / Social Media Monthly / Media Buying … are its samples); the managers build Marketing's own.
- **A template's stage**: name, team (**Which team?** — chips; the handoff has no way to set a template stage's team, which a quick stage takes from its person: an addition, flagged in the spec's gaps), **who** — *Pick at assignment* (the manager, planning each project) or **🔓 Previous stage picks** from the candidates set here (never the first stage), the handoff's fixed person exists only on quick tasks — 1–30 working days, up to three **approval levels** (a manager, 🔗 *PIC via link*, 👤 *Pick at assignment*; the PIC once and a person once a stage), **what the PIC sees on the link** (≤ 120, department words — the placeholder says so, no line under it), **subtasks** on or off, where changes go back to, the fields it fills in — a **Table**'s editor (*Edit table*) sets its columns and, on a template, **preset rows**: a cell filled in is fixed for the team, a blank one is theirs (a quick task's tables have no preset rows until the task page fills tables in — ②b).
- **🔒 Lock whole workflow**: every stage is the manager's to assign — no 🔓 hand-offs, no subtasks. The **draft keeps** each stage as it was set (unlocking gives them back); the **version** is published with the lock applied.
- **Saved as typed** (~0.7 s after a change, at once before Publish, a switch of template or leaving the page): only the parts that changed (name, lock, promise, stages), on the **draft count** (`draft_rev`) the builder last saw — a draft somebody saved since is never written over: *Not saved · X changed this template since you opened it — reload to see it.* with **Reload**, and nothing more is sent until it is. A draft may hold half-typed names and two fields of one name; publishing may not.
- **Publish v(n+1)**: the draft as its publisher saw it, checked whole (every stage, field and column named, a field once, a 🔓 stage with a stage before it and candidates, everybody named on Marketing's team now — `mkt_workflow_check`), kept whole and never changed in `mkt_workflow_versions` (who, when, the lock, the promise). *Nothing has changed since vN* refuses a second publish of the same draft. A project names the version it started on (`mkt_projects (workflow_id, workflow_version)` → a published version); the foot says how many running projects *stay on vN*.
- **The foot**: Total (working days), PIC approves n×, **Product promise** (typed in the foot — the handoff shows it but has no field for it; flagged), the handoff's warnings (*PIC approves 3+ times*, *No approval in this workflow*, *Longer than product promise (x > y days)*) and what publishing refuses, as ⚠️ chips; Publish greys with the reason in its tooltip. *Canvas template* waits for the Canvas Template Editor (③d); *Deliverables* exist in the handoff's data but not its screen — not built.
- **New Project's quick builder** draws its stages, cards and fields from the same parts (`components/mkt/builder`).

**THE MINUTES BUILDER (2026-10-23, PR ③b — mig 20261023235800).**
- **Where**: `/marketing/minutes`, MANAGER TOOLS › 📝 Minutes Builder, Marketing · Manage, on the Manager tools tab (`mkt.tracking`) as the Workflow Builder. Built before the Product Builder: a product names a minutes template as its intake.
- **A template**: its name (unique, whatever the case), its **fields** — Short text, Long text, Date, Number, People, Project link, Free space, Table (columns: Text, Number, Date, Person, 1–8; a name with *date*, *due*, *deadline* or *day* turns Text into Date), the signatures — each **Required** (blocks Complete when empty) and **PIC sees** (on the link), dragged into order; a field added goes before the signatures. **The project link and the attendees are locked**: every minutes keeps them, always required, never removed (a template has one project link and one set of signatures). Its **completion rules**: Every attendee signs, PIC signs off on the link, Must link a project, File to Library on complete, Lock after complete (their explanations as tooltips — no lines under them). A new template is the handoff's: the project, the meeting's date, the attendees, the signatures; must link a project, filed, locked.
- **Not built from the handoff's logic**: `when` (new project / monthly / a stage asks / done) and a field's `show` exist in its code but its screen draws neither; *Used by* and *n open minutes stay on vN* wait for the Product Builder and product projects (②b).
- **Saved and published as the Workflow Builder's**: only what changed, on the draft count it was made on (*Not saved · … — Reload*); **Publish v(n+1)** checks the whole template (every field and column named, a name once, the project and the attendees there) and keeps each version whole in `mkt_minutes_versions`; *Published ✓* when nothing changed since.
- **Preview** (Edit · Preview): the minutes as the staff fill them in or as the PIC reads them on the link (only the fields the PIC sees) — each field as it will ask (a typed table's dates as date inputs, the attendees from the project, signatures to click), the checks Complete makes, the buttons each side has (*Complete* / *Reopen* — *Locked · new version needed to edit*; the PIC's *Request changes* / *Approve minutes*, or *Download PDF* when the PIC only reads). Nothing typed there is kept; *Reset* clears it. The sample attendees are Marketing's own people.
- **The open template is in the URL** (`?t=`, the Workflow Builder's `?w=`) through the router: a URL changed behind its back (`history.replaceState`) is put back by the next save's refresh.

**THE PRODUCT BUILDER (2026-10-23, PR ③c — mig 20261023235900).**
- **Where**: `/marketing/products`, MANAGER TOOLS › 📦 Product Builder (between the Workflow and Minutes Builders, as the handoff draws them), Marketing · Manage, on the Manager tools tab (`mkt.tracking`). **Prices and costs are the manager's**: the reader `mkt_products()` and every write ask Manage; the staff will meet a product only when they start a product project (②b), through a reader of their own that carries no price and no cost.
- **A product**: a **code the database makes** — `PRD-<YY><NNN>` (PRD-26001), never typed (the handoff's samples carry hand-made codes, PKG-SM-A, under an *auto-generated* lock); a name, a **type** — *Recurring monthly* (a new task every month for the same team) or *One-time project* (closes when delivered) — and a description. The list: cards by status (All · Active · Draft · Retired), each with what it includes, its type, version and 🔒 price; a retired card says how many projects still run on it.
- **What's included**: categories of **actions** — each a name, a quantity (per month on a recurring product), a unit price and the **workflow** it runs on (*Link a workflow…* until picked; ↗ opens it in the Workflow Builder; **+ New action workflow** makes the action's *Standard* template there — unpublished, its note *Used by "…" in …* — and links it). A category's total, the *Sum of all actions*.
- **Intake template**: the minutes template its first meeting fills in (the Minutes Builder's).
- **Pricing** (🔒 Manager only): the list price (per month or per project; *Use sum of actions* fills it in), the internal cost (optional) and the **margin** = (price − cost) ÷ price, whole per cent, red *below 20%* (the handoff's line — a warning, never a price).
- **Saved as typed** as the other builders: only what changed, on the draft count it was made on (*Not saved · … — Reload*). The editor's five sections are ticked as the handoff's (Basics, What's included, Linked templates, Pricing, Publish).
- **Publish v(n+1)**: the day it **takes effect**, how many running projects stay on the version they started on, the version history (each version's note is what it included, in the database's words). Publishing checks it whole — named; something included; every category and action named; every action linked to a **published** workflow; a **published** intake; a price above 0; the day — keeps it whole in `mkt_product_versions` (with the workflow and minutes versions it starts projects on) and makes the product **Active**.
- **Retire** (an Active product): no new projects; those running keep their version; read-only (the database refuses its saves too) until **Reactivate**. Nothing is ever deleted (gaps).
- **A change undone before its save goes leaves nothing to send — and says *Saved*** (all three builders): the save found no difference and returned, leaving *Saving…* up for good and Publish lit (2026-10-23, found by the walk: an action added and removed within 0.7 s).

**THE CANVAS TEMPLATE EDITOR (2026-10-23, PR ③d — mig 20261024001000).**
- **Where**: the Workflow Builder's **Canvas template** tab (`/marketing/workflows`, open once a template is): the task page of a project started from that template, laid out. Saved as typed with the rest of the draft (the same count, *Not saved · … — Reload*), published with the version (`mkt_workflow_versions.canvas`), copied by Clone. The foot, the lock and Publish stay as on Workflow stages.
- **The page, top to bottom**: 📋 **Requirements** (from the intake minutes, read-only, always first — only whether the PIC sees it is set), **a section per stage** (titled by the stage, holding the fields the stage fills in), **the manager's own sections** (+ Add section — the handoff's *New section* with a Notes field filled in at the last stage; named, + Add field, removed), and the 🔒 **Free space** (always last; the only place colleagues add their own blocks).
- **A section**: 🔒 locked (colleagues can't delete it), ⭐ required before its stage submits, 👁 shown to the PIC, its icon (the handoff's twelve). A stage's section belongs to its stage; one of the manager's belongs to one stage or to **any**. A stage's section keeps its settings on the stage (`stages[i].sec`), so a stage moved or taken out takes them with it.
- **A field**: ⭐ required (a note never is), 👁 shown to the PIC, which stage fills it in (a stage's own field: its stage; the manager's: picked), a **date 📅 on the calendar** (its name there, a team's colour, the PIC's calendar or not), a **choice's options** (up to 30, each once). **A stage's own fields are the stage's** — named and typed on either tab, one field; the canvas sets how they behave. Both ⭐ stand (a field is required when its section is too), both 👁 stand (the PIC sees a field when he sees its section too).
- **The stages change, the canvas follows**: moving or taking out a stage carries the manager's sections and fields with it (by the stages' keys); a field of a stage taken out belongs to none until picked again — publishing refuses it, as a section or field left unnamed, a field twice in its section, a choice with no options (`canvasProblems` = `mkt_canvas_check`).
- **Preview**: *Staff view* — as the first of a stage's team sees the page at that stage (picked), the sections not theirs faded; *PIC link view* — only the 👁 sections and fields, the dates on their calendar, Approve · Request changes, and *Hidden: …*. A table field's blank cells take test entries, never kept.
- **Not built from the handoff** (spec gaps): its sample's table / checklist / video sections, dragging sections (its ⋮⋮ does nothing), the quick builder's Canvas tab (New Project).

**PRODUCT PROJECTS (2026-10-23, PR ②b-1 — mig 20261024002000).** From a product to the PIC's answer; what the work becomes after (the Assignment plan, billing) waits for the user's answers (open questions 10–12).
- **Started** from New Project › 📦 Product project — **the manager's** (Marketing · Manage; *Manager only* for anybody else): a subsidiary and a product **on sale today** — its newest version whose day has come, Active (`mkt_product_in_effect`; never one taking effect later, a draft or a retired product). Track ID `MKT-<SUB>-<YYMM>-<NNN>` (numbered with the subsidiary's TSK / RCR), titled "<subsidiary> · <product> · Mon YYYY", the subsidiary's PIC and WhatsApp, owned by whoever started it (the Principal, not on the roster, leaves it empty).
