# Marketing tab — the Marketing app (Marketing's view on a phone)

`/mkt-app` — `src/app/(mkt-app)/mkt-app/`, `src/components/mkt-app/`, `src/lib/mkt-app/`. The visual spec is the Claude Design handoff "Marketing View 电话版设计" (2026-11-28), copied to `.design/marketing/design_handoff_marketing_phone_app/`: README = the spec; `Marketing App.dc.html` = the prototype, its logic block the functional spec; `Marketing App Devices.dc.html` = the same app on an iPhone and an Android side by side (the file the user asked to build); `Marketing Mobile.dc.html` = stills, the pet lab, the desktop's My Space. Everything `marketing-view.md` decided holds for what the app shows — it is the same view, drawn for a phone; how its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`, its frame `../portal-wide/pwa.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

**THE APP (2026-11-28 — the user, sending the handoff: "open a worktree do this").** Marketing's view of the ONE app on a phone (pwa.md › ONE APP), as the HR app and the Finance app are HR's and Finance's — never an install of its own. It replaces the portal's phone shell on Marketing's pages (marketing-view.md › A phone keeps the portal's phone shell, superseded).
- **Who opens it** (`opensMktApp`, lib/one-app.ts): an account in Marketing's view (`isMarketingView`) holding Marketing's cell — asked before HR's, in `startPlace` and in the portal layout's phone redirect. Signed out, the sign-in comes back to it (`/login?next=/mkt-app`, the login page's `RETURNS`). Anybody else is sent to `/dashboard`. On a computer the address still opens it, a 480px column (as the HR app's).
- **The switchers** say *This app* in it and *The Marketing app* everywhere else (`phoneWhere`, lib/view-switch.ts).
- **A push** of a Marketing task or a claim opens through the start (`pushUrl` → `/?open=mkt:` / `claim:` → `mktOpenPlace`): the app on that task or request for Marketing's view on a phone, the portal page for anybody else (pwa.md › …and the Marketing app). Opened once, the address forgets it.

**WHAT IT SHOWS IS THE PORTAL'S OWN, EACH PART AS ITS PAGE (2026-11-28).** Five tabs — Tasks · Projects · ＋ · Space · More — the Task, Calendar, Chat, Notifications and Claim screens pushed over them from the right, the sheets rising from the bottom, the toast (README › Navigation model).
- Every number and line is a portal reader's, read on the server once (`loadMktAppData`, lib/mkt-app/server.ts — the board, the notices, the space, the pet, the lobby, the chat, `mkt_shell`'s counts, one's own claims, side by side through `startRead`): My Space's plate as Tasks (`plateColumns`, `weekRows`), the Drive's rows as Projects, the Calendar's events, the space, the pet and the lobby, the chat's list. lib/mkt-app/model.ts only says it in the handoff's words. A task, a visit and a chat's thread are read from the browser on the person's own move (lib/mkt-app/read.ts; the chat's `chat-read.ts`) — never a Server Action on its own (speed.md).
- **Each part is its page** (`appParts`): Projects needs the `/marketing/projects` tab, Calendar `/marketing/calendar`, Library `/marketing/library`, Meeting Rooms `/admin/rooms`, MANAGER TOOLS Marketing · Manage and the Tracking tab. A part closed is not drawn — its tab, its tile, its row.
- **Each write is the module's own Server Action** (`MktAppLive`): lib/marketing/actions.ts (`saveMktWork`, `submitMktStage`, `decideMktStage`, `reviewMktSubtask`, `nudgeMkt`, `createMktTask`, `saveMktSpace`, `careMktPet`, `sendMktChat`, `readMktChat`), the Claim page's lib/payment-requests/actions.ts, `readNotices`, `changeMyPassword`, `setViewAs`. Its button shows only where the action would let it through — `mayActOn` the pages the action itself names (`appMay`): `requireActionOn` REDIRECTS a refusal, which would throw the person out of the app.
- **＋ needs Marketing · Create** (New Project's write) and a People record — since 2026-11-28 everybody in Marketing holds it (EVERYBODY IN MARKETING OPENS A TASK, below), so ＋ is drawn for them all, as the handoff draws it; a shared login with no People record (principal@) has none.
- **Viewing as a person:** their screen, read only — the banner, no ＋, no writes (`appMay` answers none), no Me, no Claim.

**THE PUNCH AND MY PROFILE ARE ME'S (2026-11-28).** The handoff's Punch sheet (a tap and a time) and its My profile (view / edit) are drawn by Me (`components/me` — the HR app's and the Finance app's): the Tasks chip and More's punch row open Me straight on its camera (`startPunch` — the fence, the photo, a reason, as every phone in the portal punches), the profile card opens Me's hub. A login of one's own with a People record only (`loginIsPersonal`), never while viewing as.

**CLAIM AND CLAIM & APPROVE ARE THE CLAIM PAGE'S (2026-11-28).** More's 🧾 tile: one's own requests (a login of one's own, the `claims` cell, the `/claims` tab); *Claim & approve* for Claims · View at Dept, its count what waits for them. Every word of a status, a card, the hero, every rule of the form and its refusals are lib/payment-requests/model.ts's (`whereItIs`, `staffCards`, `approveCards`, `saveProblem`, `submitProblem`, `routeWords` …). The form is the New request window in one column: nothing chosen until it is picked (what it is, where a claim is paid, who bears it); one item or several; Pay to — oneself, somebody on People from the list, or a name typed (`payeePatch`); the receipts straight into the bucket as they are picked (`useRequestUploads`), taken out again when the form is left unsaved; *Save as draft* / *Submit*. A split made on the computer is kept as it is (its chip shown, not changed on the phone). A request waiting for its holder has a tick box: Approve or Reject the ticked together, a Reject's reason in the Request changes sheet. A request decided or saved stays where it was opened (the request, in its new status; a new one: the list, first).

**THE PHONE'S BACK — ONE STEP A LAYER, NESTED (2026-11-28, found writing the tests).** Each layer open holds one history step (`BackStep` → the Revenue app's `useBackStep`): a tab other than Tasks, a visit, each pushed screen, a request opened in Claim and its form over it, a teammate's thread opened from the chat's list, a sheet, Me. Back closes the top one — the handoff's ◁: a sheet, else a step inside Claim, else the pushed screen, else the visit, else Tasks. A step closed on screen is taken back a moment later, and that Back takes whatever step is on top BY THEN. So:
- **No layer hangs on another being shut.** More's tab step first stood only while nothing was pushed: a tile's screen pushed its step as the tab's went, the tab's — taken back a moment later — took the new one, and Claim's form shut by itself. The tab's step now stands under whatever opens over it; a visit's stands outside the visit drawn (one room straight to another keeps it); a visit's 💬 Message opens the chat over the visit.
- **Never two at once, never one for another.** A sheet whose button goes on somewhere — Request changes sent, the 🔓 pick, a Quick task made, Change view, 📦 Product project — closes first and goes on once its step is back (`closeSheetThen`); out of a visit straight to Tasks, the visit's step goes first, then the tab's.
- Test: `MktApp.test.tsx` › *a layer opened over another stays open*.

**THE FRAME (2026-11-28).** `position: fixed; inset: 0`, never a viewport height, the ground under it fixed too (pwa.md › A PHONE APP'S FRAME IS FIXED TO THE SCREEN; `safe-area.test.ts`); the bars on `var(--safe-bottom)`; the tab bar tucks on a scroll down (`useTuckOnScroll`); the phone's status bar takes each screen's top colour (`useStatusBar`, read off the `--mk-*` tokens); the first open's *Turn on notifications?* is the portal's (`PortalPushAsk`). Colours only through `.mkt-shell`'s tokens — the handoff's few new ones added there (`--mk-seg`, `--mk-on-ink-2`, `--mk-app-scrim`, the shadows).

**NOT BUILT FOR THE PHONE YET (2026-11-28).** A page the handoff names but does not draw opens a placeholder (its name, *This page isn't drawn for the phone yet*): Library, Meeting Rooms, Appearance, the manager tools (Tracking, the builders, Replace Person, Billing, Pool), a new product project, a Product Builder notice. The task page shows the stage's fields, its banner, its workflow strip and its steps; its tables, files, subtasks (but a delivery to review), the PIC link, posts, Free space and the full Activity are the computer's — the handoff's own *not drawn* list.

**EVERYBODY IN MARKETING OPENS A TASK (2026-11-28 — asked whether a staff member's ＋ should show, the user: "yes everyone can open task").** Marketing · Staff ticks Marketing view › Projects · Create (the cell `marketing.create`) beside its View and Edit — saved on People › Permissions as the Principal at 22:20 (template version 2 → 3; a template's ticks are data, so no migration). Its holders get ＋ on the phone — ⚡ Quick task and 🔁 Recurring task; 📦 Product project stays Marketing · Manage's (*Manager only*) — and **+ New Project** on the computer, where a task's stages may name anybody on Marketing's roster and their approvers (each stage's person accepts it; Tracking shows it all). That day none of the template's nine people had a login yet: nobody's screen changed until HR invites them.
