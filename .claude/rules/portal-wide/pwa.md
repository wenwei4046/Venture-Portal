# Every tab — the installed app (PWA)

Installing the portal, a release reaching apps left open, the phone's own navigation bar. Read this before changing any of them, and again in every full scan of such a change (CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab). CLAUDE.md › Coding Conventions keeps the summary. Until 2026-10-10 these were the INSTALLABLE paragraph of the `(member)/member/` tree comment and two paragraphs of Coding Conventions, moved word for word.

**INSTALLABLE (2026-09-23):** the whole portal is a PWA — ONE app, `start_url` `/` (which already routes member → /member, everybody else → /dashboard). `public/manifest.webmanifest` is linked BY HAND in the root layout's <head>: an `app/manifest.ts` gets streamed into <body> and Chrome then refuses to install. `public/sw.js` caches NOTHING but `offline.html` — every page is somebody's money, and a cached copy would outlive the session on a shared phone. Install entry points: login card, member Profile row, Principal Profile card (`components/pwa/`, `lib/pwa/install.ts`). Icons from `tools/generate-pwa-icons.mjs`.

A release reaches open apps (2026-10-06) — an installed app is resumed from memory, not reloaded, and the member app changes screens with `history.pushState` and reads through Server Actions, so it never makes the navigation that would meet a new build; and Next recalculates every Server Action's id per build, so a copy left open across a release starts failing its reads. The service worker is NOT the cause (it caches no page). The build names itself: `next.config.ts` bakes `VERCEL_DEPLOYMENT_ID || VERCEL_GIT_COMMIT_SHA` into the bundle as `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_VERSION` and answers it on `/sw.js` as `X-App-Version` (empty off Vercel = never prompts). `components/pwa/UpdatePrompt` (root layout) asks with a plain HEAD — no deployment pin, so Skew Protection cannot keep it on the old build — on load, on every return to the screen (`visibilitychange`, `pageshow`), on `online` and every 5 min in view; a newer build is OFFERED (*A new version is ready* · Update · ×), and IN THE REVENUE APP ONLY (`/member`, `mayReloadOn`) after 30+ min away it reloads on return unless a field still had the focus or something was typed in the 10 min before leaving (`lib/app-update.ts`, user chose A 2026-10-06). The portal is only ever offered it: HR › Payroll holds edits until *Save all* without a `beforeunload` guard, and edits made long before a tab switch are invisible to any typing check — a reload there could cost unsaved pay. The member app's quote survives a reload (tab `sessionStorage`). Nothing reloads by itself while on screen. DESIGN.md › Update prompt.

The phone's navigation bar after a reload (2026-10-10) — the installed app draws under the phone's own buttons (`viewport-fit=cover`) and its bottom bars stood on `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; after a reload (Update, a stale-action pull, Try again) on an Android phone with three-button navigation the Revenue app's bar sat BEHIND those buttons, only its top edge and the Quote circle showing (user's screenshot) — by its geometry the page still drawn under them and the inset back at 0 (reproduced with CDP `Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride`: 48 → reload at 0 gives the same picture). Chrome's edge-to-edge controller does not re-push an unchanged bottom inset for a new document and returns early for a tappable three-button bar (read in Chromium's `EdgeToEdgeControllerImpl`, not seen on the device). So every bottom bar, and what pads itself clear of one, uses `var(--safe-bottom)` = max(the browser's inset, `--safe-bottom-floor`): `components/pwa/SafeAreaMemory` (root layout) reads the real inset off a probe, remembers a non-zero one with its window in `sessionStorage` (`vp-safe-bottom`), and a page that has heard only 0 in exactly that window stands on it — set before the first paint by the head script `SAFE_BOTTOM_BOOT`, lifted the moment the browser speaks (`lib/safe-area.ts`). A fresh launch, a desktop and a phone with no bar are untouched. `src/lib/safe-area.test.ts` refuses a new bare `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` outside the previews. The Revenue app's SHEETS reach the screen's bottom edge too (`Sheet.tsx` `.panel`, `.psPanel` + its bed-frame `.pickPanel`, Notifications `.page`, View as `.va`): each carries a transparent `border-bottom: var(--sheet-safe)` (shell.module.css `.screen`) = only what its drawn 24–34px bottom padding does not cover — iPhone `--safe-bottom − 30px` (34 → 4px, looks as drawn), Android `− 6px` (three buttons 48 → 42px, clear by 12–28px) — taken out of the sheet's height so it covers no more of the screen. DESIGN.md › The phone's own navigation bar. HR's kit (`components/hrv`, 2026-10-11): HR's frame runs on under the 54px tab bar on a phone, so its drawers and their scrims end at `54px + 10px + var(--safe-bottom)`, a window centres above the bar and is never taller than the room there, and the toast stands `54px + 30px + var(--safe-bottom)` up (`components/hrv/phone-overlays.test.ts`; the parity audit found + New request's Submit to HR under the bar).

**THE HR APP INSTALLS AS ITS OWN (2026-10-11, the HR revamp's second package — the user: "HR app at /hr-app, installable") — UNDONE the same day, see ONE APP below:** `/hr-app` has its own manifest, `public/hr-app.webmanifest` (`id` / `start_url` / `scope` `/hr-app`, *HOUZS HR*, short name *HR*, the portal's icons). The root layout's hand-written `<link rel="manifest">` stays the portal's; the HR app swaps its `href` on mount and puts it back on the way out (`components/hr-app/HrAppManifest.tsx`) — Chrome re-reads a manifest whose link changes, and iOS reads the link when *Add to Home Screen* is pressed. Signed out, the app sends you to `/login?next=/hr-app`, the only return the sign-in honours. `sw.js` is unchanged (still `offline.html` only). The rules: `../hr/hr-app.md`.

**ONE APP (2026-10-11 — the user: "他们都是一个 app，我要的就是 portal app"):** one install for everybody, this manifest's — never a second one (`src/lib/one-app.test.ts` refuses another `.webmanifest` in `public/` or any other named in code). Which face opens is `lib/one-app.ts`: a Revenue member the Revenue app; HR's view on a PHONE the HR app (`/` and every portal page redirect it — the portal's HR pages are the computer's); everybody else the portal, until Finance's and the Principal's phone faces join this same app. The rules: `../hr/hr-app.md` › ONE APP.

**PHONE NOTIFICATIONS (2026-10-11 — the user: "现在就做", "铃铛 + 手机推送"; mig `20261011180000`, plan `.design/hr-r2/PLAN-H.md`).**
- **What is told, to whom:** a hiring request's step → the person who asked (acknowledged, more information, declined, filled, cancelled); a meeting → the people in it (booked for you, added, changed, taken off, cancelled — never whoever did it); HR's new work → whoever holds its TOPIC (a guest-link manpower request, a guest-link booking made / changed / cancelled, a member's claim / waiting list / replacement sent or answered). AFTER triggers write `app_notifications` beside the event, in its transaction; a trigger that fails only warns, so a notice never stops the work it tells of.
- **Topics, not other people's permissions:** `current_actions()` answers for the caller only, so the HR app writes its viewer's topics on each load (`notification_topics_sync`, `hrTopics` in lib/hr-app/server.ts) and the database keeps only those their own actions hold (`hiring.approve`, `rooms.edit`, `member_is_manager('member_requests_manage')`); ANY permission change (templates, their grants or tabs, a person's cells or boxes, a title's template, a staff row's title / login / status, an account's role / active) forgets EVERY topic until each account opens the HR app again. Never while viewing as.
- **Push without the service role:** VAPID (RFC 8292) — the public key is a constant (`lib/push/config.ts`), the private half is `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` in the server's environment only (Vercel, `.env.local`), never in the repo. The outbox (`push_outbox_take` / `push_outbox_done`) asks a secret made from that key (HMAC, `outboxSecret`), whose sha256 is `push_config`'s one row; it is granted to anon AND authenticated because the guest page's server actions use the publishable key — refused without the secret. Messages are RFC 8291 `aes128gcm`, WebCrypto only (`lib/push/webpush.ts`, held to the RFC's own example byte for byte). With no private key nothing is pushed and the bells still work.
- **The key is three places, changed together (2026-10-11 — set on Vercel Production, Sensitive, and redeployed).** `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` (Vercel), the public half in `lib/push/config.ts`, and `push_config.outbox_secret_sha256` (a new migration) are one set: change the private key alone and pushing stops WITHOUT an error (the outbox refuses the secret; push services refuse the signature). A new pair also means every phone turns Phone notifications on again — their subscriptions are bound to the old public key. A Sensitive variable is never shown again, so Vercel is no backup: the readable copy is the `.env.local` it was made in — the user keeps it in a password manager. Check a pair without printing it: the private scalar's public point must equal the constant, and sha256 of `outboxSecret(key)` the stored row.
- **When:** `pushSoon()` after each write that can tell somebody something (rooms book / cancel on all three surfaces, hiring's writes, the guest page's book / cancel / request, a member's request) — `after()`, so nobody waits on a push service; the hourly `/api/member-queue` sweep takes what is left. A device the push service calls gone (404 / 410) is forgotten; a notice is pushed for a day at most and kept 90.
- **The phone:** `public/sw.js` shows each push (its tag replaces a repeat) and a tap opens its url in an open window of that app, else a new one — `/hr-app?open=hire:HR-205` / `booking:…` / `req:…` in the HR app, `/member?page=account&view=rooms` or `/member?bell=1` in the Revenue app (`noticeUrl`). The permission is asked ONLY when the person presses — *Phone notifications* (HR app › More; Revenue app › Profile), or the first open's question below — `components/pwa/push-client.ts`. An iPhone shows them only for an app added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+): the row says so.
- **The first open asks (2026-10-11 — the user: "当我第一次点进去这个 app … 跳上来一个窗口，问我可不可以 allow notification"):** `components/pwa/PushAsk.tsx`, in the HR app (not while viewing a person) and the Revenue app (not the Principal's, not the demo) — a centred card, *Turn on notifications?*, **Allow notifications** (the press the phone's own prompt needs) and **Not now**. Shown once per phone (`vp-push-asked` in localStorage; a phone that cannot store it is not asked again and again), only while the phone's notifications are off and its browser was never asked. Turning them on any time stays in the app's row.
- **Every open signs up again a phone that already said yes (`healPush`):** a sign-up the server never heard of is told now, and a phone that allowed them but holds no sign-up is signed up without asking. `push_subscribe` upserts by address, so it costs one call when all is well. Found the same day: the user's switch "turned on" three times and nothing ever reached the server.
- **Whatever goes wrong is said, and told to the server:** the browser's own error on the screen ("This phone could not sign up for notifications (AbortError: …)"), and `reportPushTrouble` writes it, with the stage (`permission` / `worker` / `subscribe` / `save` / `heal`) and the browser, to Vercel's runtime log as `[push] trouble` — nothing is stored. `push_subscribe`'s refusals go there as `[push] push_subscribe refused`. Subscribing waits for the ACTIVE service worker (`navigator.serviceWorker.ready`, 8 s): the first open of a fresh install is when it is not yet active.
- **The status-bar icon is `public/icons/badge-96.png` — the H alone, white on transparent** (`tools/generate-pwa-icons.mjs`; `sw.js` `badge`). Android keeps only a badge's alpha: the app tile there showed as a solid white square on the user's first test push (2026-10-11). The tile stays the notification's `icon`.
- **A tap ALWAYS goes through `clients.openWindow(url)` — never an open window** (`sw.js` `notificationclick`). Chrome on Android hands a url inside an installed app's scope to that app and makes it go there even when it is already open (`ServiceTabLauncher`: `findFirstWebApkPackage`, `forceNavigation = true`); an iPhone opens it in the Home Screen app, the only place its pushes arrive. Reusing an open window went wrong twice on 2026-10-11: loading the notice's page before focusing let the moment a tap allows for focusing pass (the tap seemed to do nothing), and focusing the most recently used window brought a **Chrome tab** forward instead of the app ("它跳进去是进了我的 Chrome，而不是进了我的 Apps") — `clients.matchAll` lists the browser's tabs of the site too, and nothing tells a tab from the installed app. `src/lib/pwa/service-worker.test.ts` runs `sw.js` in a sandbox and keeps it.
- **Sending one by hand (a test):** insert one `app_notifications` row for the person, then flush the outbox from a PC exactly as the server does — the publishable key + the outbox secret (HMAC of `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`), never the service role; Node 24 runs the repo's own `src/lib/push/webpush.ts` directly. The hourly sweep (`/api/member-queue`, :00) would send it too. Delete the test row after.

**THE BOTTOM BAR TUCKS AWAY ON A SCROLL DOWN (2026-10-11 — the user: "如果电话版本的话，我要它下面那个 bar，如果我滑上去的时候，它是会收下来的 … 等这个爬升来的时候，它才可以又再跑起来"):** one hook, `components/pwa/tuck.ts` — `useTuckOnScroll(barRef, enabled, resetKey)` — and `tuck.module.css` (`.tuckable`, `data-tucked`), on all three phone bars: the HR app's tabs, the Revenue app's nav (the live app only) and the portal's `BottomTabBar` (not while More is open). Down by more than 24px tucks it, up by 24px brings it back, as does a page's top (under 48px) or another page (`resetKey`: the HR app's page — its tab and every page pushed on it, `hr-app/page-scroll.ts` `pageKeyOf`; the Revenue app's screen, Profile page, Insight and Arena views and board; the portal's path). What a change must keep:
- **On entering, the bar is there** (the user: "点进去的时候理应能看到下面的 bar，只有滑下去的时候才看不到"). A new page starts the count afresh — `resetKey` is in the listener's effect, so its run and positions start over; a run carried over from the page before tucked it at the first pixel of the next. Coming back to the app (`visibilitychange` → visible, `pageshow`) shows it too: a phone resumes the app as it was left.
- **A tucked bar is `inert`** — nothing hidden can be tapped or focused.
- **An in-flow bar keeps `--safe-bottom` of its height in the flow** (margin `--safe-bottom − --bar-h`, the transform takes the rest away). Giving back the whole height put a page's last lines under Android's buttons for good: scrolling up to see them brings the bar back. Docks and action bars between the page and the bar come down onto that strip; the Revenue app paints it white (`.screenLive`'s second background layer), whatever `--status-bg` is.
- **The browser pulling a page back from its foot is not a scroll up.** As the frame grows into the bar's space, a page at its foot is pulled back (`dy < 0` landing on the new foot) — read as a scroll up, the bar flickered at every page's end. Positions are clamped to [0, max], so iOS's bounce past either end is standing at it.
- **A page that would have less than 48px of room once the bar is gone never tucks it** — a page that cannot scroll leaves no way to bring the bar back.
- **A scroll inside `[role="dialog"]`, `[inert]` or `[aria-hidden="true"]` moves nothing** — a sheet, a drawer, a page sliding over: the bar is under it; and the Revenue app keeps its closed pages mounted (the first phone check scrolled the closed View as list, and the bar left an unmoved Today). A page that slides over the screen says `role="dialog"` (View as gained it, as Notifications had).
- **Whatever stands on the portal's tab bar reads `--tabbar-h`, never a bare 54px** — 54px, 0 under `html[data-tabbar-tucked]` (set by `BottomTabBar` while tucked): Finance's `.bulk-bar`; the HR view's `.hrv-frame`, whose sheet scrolls inside itself with its foot always in view, and that frame's drawers, scrims, windows and toasts (`hrv.module.css`; `hrv/phone-overlays.test.ts`). A page whose content simply runs on (`PortalFrame`'s 54 + 24px, `.sheet-frame`'s 54 + 10px) keeps its bottom padding — only the end of its content.
