# Revenue tab — the CRM

WhatsApp and Messenger (Meta's webhooks — the second machine system, CLAUDE.md › Coding Conventions › Machine ingest), the CRM's reads and writes, the redesign (the salesperson CRM and the Control panel) and its permissions.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 these were paragraphs of CLAUDE.md › Coding Conventions (Machine ingest, the CRM redesign, the CRM and the permission matrix), moved word for word.

**THE SECOND SYSTEM: META (user-approved 2026-10-01 for Messenger, 2026-10-02 for WhatsApp under the same approval).** `app/api/whatsapp/v1/webhook` (GET = Meta's one-time handshake, answered only when `hub.verify_token` equals `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`; POST = deliveries signed `X-Hub-Signature-256`, HMAC of the RAW body with `META_APP_SECRET`, both compared in constant time and failing closed when unset) → the one ingest RPC `crm_ingest_whatsapp` (service role only; writes `crm_contacts` / `crm_messages`, logs every delivery in `crm_channel_events`); excluded from `middleware.ts`; `GET /api/whatsapp/v1/health` reports which settings are set, yes/no only. The crm_* tables have RLS on (mig `20261002010000`, PGlite check `tests/member-sql/validate-crm-whatsapp.mjs`).

**The CRM's read path (2026-10-02, migs `20261002070000` / `080000` / `090000`, PGlite check `validate-crm-team.mjs`):** SELECT only, writes stay closed. A person's CRM role comes from their TITLE through `crm_role_map` (empty until the owner maps titles — until then nobody but the service role reads a customer); who is below whom is `hr_staff_costs.reports_to`. Everyone reads themselves and their whole downline, never a peer; Boss and HOD read everything, including customers no team took; a TL also reads their team's not-yet-assigned enquiries (`crm_contacts.team_id`).

**The Principal is the Boss (2026-10-03, mig `20261003070000`):** an account with no staff record of its own that IS the Principal (`users.role = 'principal'`, as `member_principal_snapshot`) is, to `crm_me()`, the active staff member whose title maps to Boss (the CEO) — so the Boss reviews the CRM from the Principal login; the portal's view-as `staff=` does not change who the CRM thinks you are. A linked record always wins.

**Principal vs HOD (user 2026-10-03, mig `20261003080000`, PGlite `validate-crm-assign.mjs` / `validate-crm-writes.mjs`):** the Principal (Boss) takes and answers no enquiries — CRM tabs Overview (leads, appt rate, show-up rate, each team; `MemberCrmOverview`) · Assignment · Calendar, no Inbox/Contacts; picking a member in the Revenue app's Today opens the CRM on that member (`viewingStaffId`). The HOD sells too: Inbox/Contacts/Calendar with All · Mine · Teams, replies only to customers given to them (`crm_can_reply`; `crm_give_to_hod`, from a chat's With… control). Both set full time and decide ads ad by ad on Assignment (`MemberCrmAssignment`, explicit Save → `crm_save_ad_rule(ad, label, tls, shares)`): hand to teams (evenly) or split by % (`crm_assign_rules.shares`, adding to 100). An ad nobody set, and an enquiry from no ad, goes EVENLY across every team (`crm_pick_team`; `crm_team_settings.team_share` and Mode 2 are no longer read; the next-10 preview is gone). The signed-in side may only ask about ITSELF (`crm_my_role()`, `crm_my_downline()`; the any-person `crm_role_of` / `crm_downline` are service-role only), and every policy wraps them as `(select …)` so they run once per query. `src/lib/member/crm-team.ts` is the TypeScript twin — keep them in step. New: `crm_role_map`, `crm_staff` (full_time, the HOD's call), `crm_assign_rules` (ad → TLs), `crm_team_settings` (team %; which Mon–Thu days go by the member %s — an unticked day waits in the TL's To assign list; a Fri–Sun switch: on = full-time RS only in turn, off = the same %s — mig `20261003010000`, user 2026-10-03; the old `weekday` column is unread), `crm_appointments`. `crm_people()` (mig `20261002100000`) = who you are + the names/roles/upline/full-time of the people you may see (a salesperson cannot read hr_staff_costs; never a salary). The member app's CRM reads all of it LIVE under the member's own session (`app/(member)/member/crm-actions.ts` → `lib/member/crm-live.ts`, rows → the demo's shapes); `/member/preview` keeps the demo.

**Live writes (2026-10-03, mig `20261003030000`, PGlite `validate-crm-writes.mjs`):** every change is a database function under the member's own session that asks who may first — mark read, status/Lost reason, book/move/came/cancel, a TL assigning in their team, the Boss/HOD sending to a team, team days/switch/%s, the split between teams, ad → TLs, full time (HOD). `crmWrite` (crm-actions.ts) passes calls through from a fixed list; `MemberCrm.tsx` `persist` saves FIRST and changes the screen only on yes (settings save 0.6 s after the last change). Live sends the customer nothing about appointments yet and says so.

**Showrooms (2026-10-03, mig `20261003050000`):** `crm_showrooms` is the booking list (Kuala Lumpur today, id `kl`); the Boss / HOD add, rename, close or reopen one in the CRM's Settings (`crm_save_showroom`), and `crm_book` / `crm_move_appointment` take only an open one. The screen names showrooms through `components/member/crm-format.ts` (`setShowroomList`), never the demo's list on live.

**New enquiries are dealt on arrival** inside `crm_ingest_whatsapp` (mig `20261003020000`, PGlite `validate-crm-assign.mjs`; twin `pickInTeam`), balanced on the last 30 days. Not yet live: photos/voice, templates, auto-replies.

**Built: replying on WhatsApp (mig `20261002110000`).** `sendCrmReply` (crm-actions.ts), under the member's OWN session — no service role: `crm_can_reply(contact)` first (the customer's own person or an upline who takes enquiries / a Senior TL; never Boss or HOD; a waiting enquiry is its TL's), Meta's 24-hour window (`lib/whatsapp/send.ts` `windowOpen`, from `crm_contacts.last_customer_at`), then the Cloud API with `META_SYSTEM_USER_TOKEN` (server env, never the browser), then `crm_record_reply` (asks again; keeps Meta's wamid so the webhook's sent/delivered/read/failed find it; New → Follow up). Text only; photos, voice and templates come later. The message shows only after it went AND was kept; a failed send keeps the draft.

**Messenger (2026-10-03)** is the second endpoint of this same system: `app/api/messenger/v1/webhook` (same handshake — `MESSENGER_VERIFY_TOKEN`, else `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN` — and the same signature), our Pages only (`META_PAGE_IDS`), the customer's name asked of Meta with the Page's token before the one ingest RPC `crm_ingest_messenger` (mig `20261003040000`, PGlite `validate-crm-messenger.mjs`): one customer per (Page, PSID), dealt on arrival, deliveries by mid and reads by watermark. Replies (`sendCrmReply`) use the Page's token from the System User token: inside 24 h a normal response, 24 h–7 days with the HUMAN_AGENT tag (Meta must have approved Human Agent), after that none. Customer media: `GET /member/crm-media/<message id>` (read-only download; the message read under the member's session, the file fetched from Meta's own hosts with the token — `lib/whatsapp/media.ts`).

**THE DEALER, REV 1011 (2026-10-11, mig `20261011130000`; user: "A B C D OK"):** overrides the dealing rules above where they differ.
