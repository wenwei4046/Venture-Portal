# Revenue tab — the Product List, costing and the member catalogue

The Product List (`/member/products`, a page of the Revenue app), combos, the ERP catalogue mirror (items only), the one cost rule, option charges, and what the Quote sells and how it costs it.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 these were paragraphs of the Architecture tree's `revenue/` comment and the section *Member catalogue & margin cost basis* in CLAUDE.md, moved word for word.

## The Product List

**THE PRODUCT LIST IS A PAGE OF THE REVENUE APP (2026-10-08, user: 把 product list 直接搬过去 revenue view … 只有拥有权限进去的时候，才会看到这个 tab):** `/member/products`, under the route group `(member)/member/(app)` — its layout renders `RevenueApp` around the page (`(app)/page.tsx` is null, `(app)/loading.tsx` keeps the shell up between the two). The app's sidebar shows it under PRINCIPAL only to whoever holds `product_list` (`lib/member/product-access.ts`; a Principal in ANY view — Revenue View, or an HR / Finance view left on in the portal — is asked about the account itself, `getAccountUser()`, and the Save stands behind the same answer, `requireProductList`: an HR view used to hide the row with nothing saying why), the Module switcher beside it (Principal only, `setViewAs`). The portal's `/revenue/products` redirects there and the portal nav lost its row. Drawn to the design handoff (`.design/revenue-app/design_handoff_product_list`, `_components/products.module.css`; `tools/measure-pl.mjs` diffs every element against the prototype — box-sizing and the browser's own input padding are restored where Tailwind's reset changed them). Its code never reaches a member's bundle: `components/member/cost-basis-wording.test.ts` exempts the folder and refuses an import of it. A save `router.refresh()`es the layout, so the Quote's catalogue follows at once. Code moved from `revenue/products` + `revenue/catalogue-actions.ts` to `(app)/products/` (`actions.ts`).

**COMBOS ARE OURS (2026-10-08, user: need delete button, then that side no need sync with houzs erp anymore):** `catalogue_combos` holds every combo — the ERP's 76 live rows adopted once (`erp_id`, same model, compartments, tier, name, heights and effective date, so no bill changed), + Add combo makes more (`catalogue_add_combo`), Edit prices › Remove takes a set away (`catalogue_remove_combos`: every tier, and its prices deleted; the row kept with `removed_at` / `removed_by`; 42501 without admin scope, 22023 a set already gone). `catalogue_live_combos` — read by `catalogue_apply_combos` (the run) and `member_catalogue` (the Quote) — is `catalogue_combos` alone; `erp_sofa_combos` is still written by the ingest and read by nothing, so the ERP needs no change. A bill costed at a removed combo keeps that cost until its month is Recosted. A PIECE MAY BE EITHER SIDE (same day, user: 我需要左右都算的 function): after a module whose other side the model also has (`1A(R)(LHF)` / `(RHF)`, `2A-LHF` / `-RHF`), Add combo offers the pair as one more card — `1A(R)` · *LHF or RHF* — and each piece of it is ONE slot of both sides, the ERP's own shape, so a set adopted from the ERP can be made again after a Remove (`moduleSide` / `comboPieces` in `_components/add-combo.ts`; a pair adds up at the dearer side). `catalogue_add_combo` takes 1–4 modules per slot, none twice; the costing already matched a slot by any of them. ONE BILL, ONE COMBO (same day, user chose 完全一样才算): a combo costs a bill only when the sofa IS its set — a 3+2+1 bill takes 3+2+1, never 3+2, and with 3+2 alone takes none (`catalogue_apply_combos`, twin `pickCombo`; 14 of the 67 bills since August that the ERP's rule would have matched were sets with a piece more). Add combo refuses a set one bill could match as well as an existing one — `1A(R) (LHF or RHF) + 1NA` beside `1A(R)(LHF) + 1NA` (`catalogue_combos_collide`, twin `combosCollide`); none of the 62 adopted sets collide. Migs `20261008010000` – `040000`.

**THE CATALOGUE, MIRRORED — ITEMS ONLY (2026-09-24):** `products/` (Product List) is the Houzs ERP's Products page mirrored item by item — SKU Master, Modular (models + their allowed options), Maintenance (Bedframe + Sofa pools and specials, the fabrics and their PRICE_1/2/3 tier — NOT "Products Maintenance"), Combo Pricing — with a fifth tab, Costing gaps. Not mirrored: Variants, Fabric Converter, Categories. **User rule: the mirror syncs ITEMS, never a price or a cost; every price is typed HERE and every margin in the portal — the member calculator, the run, the drill-downs — is measured against it.** Two halves, and the split is the rule: `erp_*` (`erp_products`, `erp_product_models`, `erp_maintenance_options`, `erp_fabrics`, `erp_sofa_combos` — unread since 2026-10-08, combos are ours) is what the ERP says EXISTS, written only by `ingest_erp_catalogue` (service role, money stripped again on the way in AT ANY DEPTH — a price tier is a label, not money — `full: true` retires what a carried section omits as `removed_at` — never deletes; an empty section retires nothing); `catalogue_*` (`catalogue_skus` price / sofa `price_grid` / `offered`, `catalogue_models` offered + `enabled` option ticks + gift override + section / brand picks (read ONLY for a mattress or sofa the ERP gives no brand, 2026-10-06 — see Member catalogue below), `catalogue_option_prices`, `catalogue_combo_prices` keyed on (base model, slots, tier)) is what the PORTAL decides and no delivery touches. A SKU the ERP adds arrives UNticked. First sight inherits the old hand-keyed list's price (`catalogue_adopt_legacy`: ERP bridge code, own code, then for a mattress brand + model + size when unique both ways — mig `20260924025000`; bed frames were keyed by SUPPLIER codes the ERP does not have, so they wait for a person).

**ONE COST RULE (mig `20260924030000`):** `catalogue_line_cost()` = the SKU's portal price (a sofa module's grid cell at the line's seat height × fabric tier, else its one figure) × qty — no option adds to it since 2026-10-06 (every option priced on Maintenance is a CHARGE, below); the options read from the line's `raw.variants` when the feed carries them, else parsed from the ERP's own `description2` grammar (`catalogue_line_facts`). Then, at COMMIT (deferred trigger), a sofa build that IS a priced combo's set (exactly, since 2026-10-08 — the ERP took one it merely covered) is costed at the combo, spread over its modules by their own costs (the combo always wins; one tier + one height; uniform qty or no combo). The ERP's own line cost is NOT a fallback: nothing priced = no cost = no margin, and *Costing gaps* says why (`not_in_sku_master` / `unpriced` / `no_height`). `recost_sales_order_lines` RE-PRICES a month from the Product List (23RUN kept); a month already landed changes only when somebody presses Recost, and the run must be computed again. The TypeScript twins are `lib/catalogue/*` — the calculator and the run cannot disagree about a sofa.

**A BED FRAME'S OPTIONS ARE CHARGED, NOT COSTED (2026-10-05):** a bed frame's divan, leg, GAP and total height priced on Maintenance above 0 is what the CUSTOMER pays on top (user: 16 寸需要 100 块 … 不会算进 margin … 只会给普通 commission): `sales_order_lines.option_charge_amount` = qty × charges, filled with the cost (fill-cost trigger, Recost). OUT of the margin on both sides — `marginBase` / `revenue_month_orders.margin_revenue` / `member_order_facts.goods_revenue` take it off the revenue, the cost never had it — IN the sales the rate is paid on (`commissionable`, and the director threshold reads `commissionable`, not margin revenue), never KPI. Not set = FOC (total height too, user 2026-10-05); specials stay a cost. Maintenance says *Charge (RM)* for those pools. The Quote offers one ONLY while it is turned ON for that model on Modular (user 2026-10-05: 选择 Turn ON 的他才会 出现) — ON + charged = "+ RM x", ON without a charge = FOC, not ON = never offered, as a SKU or model not ON is never offered. `lineCharge` (`lib/catalogue/pricing.ts`), migs `20261005010000` / `020000`.

**EVERY OPTION PRICE IS A CHARGE (2026-10-06):** "我要那些价钱是不会算进 margin 的，加 100 就一定 default calculator 100" — a bed frame's SPECIALS and a sofa's LEG HEIGHT and SPECIALS priced on Maintenance are the customer's too, out of the margin, in the commission, never KPI; nothing is a cost surcharge any more (`catalogue_line_surcharge` answers 0; a price is never negative). A bed frame per frame, on its line. A SOFA ONCE PER SOFA ("整组 sofa 只加一次"): at commit, beside the combos, `catalogue_apply_sofa_charges` groups an order's sofa lines by base model, charges each distinct leg / special any piece names once × sets (the smallest line qty: ×2 everywhere = two sofas) and spreads it over the pieces by their sales (`cost_detail.charge_set`); Recost does the same. The Quote: a sofa's leg = FOC + the charged legs, as a bed frame's; a SPECIAL is offered only when charged, "+ RM x" (user, same day: "if there is no price … just don't show it") — ON on Modular with no price = not shown, and the server refuses it on a bed frame; step 2 and the customer quote say *Option charges*. `sofaSetCharges` is the twin. Mig `20261006050000`. `catalogueModelsOf` (`lib/member/priced-catalogue.ts`, the ONE reader of the models for the snapshot AND the quote) keeps each kind's charges — a bed frame's heights + specials, a sofa's leg + specials; until 2026-10-06 it kept a bed frame's heights only, so the sofa's Leg height row never showed. The ERP pushes the catalogue to `POST /api/erp/v1/products` (contract §9, v2) on the sales-order feed's key, whole, only when its digest changed. `lib/erp-sync/catalogue.ts`, `(member)/member/(app)/products/actions.ts`, migs `20260924020000` / `025000` / `030000`.

## Member catalogue & margin cost basis (2026-09-12, catalogue 2026-09-24)

The member Quote page sells from the Product List, not a list of its own: `member_catalogue()` (mig `20260924040000`) answers every SKU ticked on **Product List › Modular** (`catalogue_skus.offered`, and its model's `catalogue_models.offered`), still ACTIVE in the ERP, under a brand the member's calculator scheme sells — "10 SKUs, 7 ticked → the calculator offers 7" (user 2026-09-24). Both snapshots read it UNPRICED (names, sizes, the sofa grid's height × tier choices, each sofa / bed frame model's dropdowns = the portal's ticks ∩ what the ERP still allows ∩ the live Maintenance pool) and return `catalogueModels`; the quote reads it PRICED through the service role only (`lib/member/server.ts`, after `member_quote_context` authorised the session) — no price ever reaches a browser. The cart carries ONE sofa build (seat height, tier, leg, specials — the Revenue app's sofa sheet offers the leg and specials again since 2026-10-06, only what Modular turned ON for EVERY sofa model in the cart AND Maintenance charges; the revamp had left them out: `sofaPick` / `sofaBuildToSend` in `components/member/app/quote/quote-model.ts`); since 2026-10-05 EACH bed frame line carries its own build (`QuoteLine.bedframe`): its divan, leg, gap and total height are FOC or one of the options turned ON for the model on Modular that Maintenance CHARGES and the ERP still allows (`MemberCatalogueModel.charges` — a customer's price, so the unpriced snapshot carries it), its specials the same minus the FOC — a special with no price is not offered at all (user 2026-10-06); one frame built two ways is two lines (`frameBuildError`, `lib/member/build.ts`), and the server refuses anything else. The keypad is the SELLING price: bought KPI items and the option charges ride on top, out of the margin (a FREE KPI item comes off the price), and `QuoteResult.customerPays` says the total. The calculator costs the way the run does (`lib/member/calculator.ts` `costLines` over `lib/catalogue/*`): SKU price at the build, a matched priced combo costed as the combo; every option priced on Maintenance is CHARGED on top instead (a bed frame's per frame, a sofa's once per sofa — `sofaSetCharges`, 2026-10-06). People › Member Services › Product options is now a pointer to Modular (`comm_products` / `member_product_options` are no longer read by the member app). **Riders**: bed frames and accessories carry NO brand — they join any cart, their cost counts in the one margin, and the mattress or sofa's brand decides the ladder (in the run: the bill's own brand, the ERP's Branding — `SalesOrder.brandCode` into `marginBase`, 2026-09-27) — and so does a brandless mattress or sofa marked *Rider* on Modular (`catalogue_models.brand_code = 'RIDER'`, read as no brand, never as one). A mattress or sofa is never a rider by default: one whose ERP branding matches no brand (MYLATEX, or none — 36 mattresses + 6 sofas on 2026-10-06) is not offered until a Margin ladder is chosen for it, and Modular says so (`isRiderModel`, `lib/catalogue/options.ts`). **Modular asks ONLY there (2026-10-06, user: 它是跟着 brand，而不是跟着我这里去选 … for those system can't detect the brand):** a model's window shows *Margin ladder* and *Calculator section* only for such a mattress or sofa (`needsBrandPick`); a branded one follows its brand and its ERP section, and a bed frame / accessory / every other category is always a rider in its ERP section (user: its margin follows the calculator's or the sales order's brand). `member_catalogue` and the Product List loader ignore a ladder or section stored for either; mig `20261006170000` cleared the 357 sections the September carry-over had stored (22 accessories' Pillow / Protector from the old list now read the ERP's Accessory / Protector — all gift-eligible, one accessory list in the Revenue app). A model's dropdown with an EMPTY ERP list is the whole Maintenance pool — the ERP's own "no restriction" (`erpAllows`). **Cost basis**: Revenue Team cost = cost ÷ `comm_schemes.revenue_cost_divisor` (Fair 0.7; Rules › Scheme rules) in the calculator, the snapshot order margins and the run alike. Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price; the price for threshold t is cost ÷ (1 − t), never cost × (1 + t). Mig `20260915060000`.
