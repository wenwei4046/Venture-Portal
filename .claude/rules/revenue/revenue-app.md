# Revenue tab — the Revenue app (`/member`)

Today, Insight, Quote, Arena, Profile; requests and claims (and HR's side of them, People › Member services); My income, honours, brand weeks; how the app reads and refreshes — `src/app/(member)/member/`, `src/components/member/`, `src/lib/member/`. The Product List page inside the app is `product-list.md`, the CRM is `crm.md`, installing the app is `../portal-wide/pwa.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 this was the Architecture tree's comment on `(member)/member/` in CLAUDE.md, moved word for word.

## `src/app/(member)/member/`

(member)/member/ ← the Revenue member app: one phone-shaped workspace for a salesperson, NOT the portal. Its own design language (warm editorial, `components/member/member.module.css`), its own nav: Today / Income / Quote / Team / **Profile** (was Account until 2026-09-13; Insight lives INSIDE it, first card, and `page=insight` keeps Profile lit). `preview/` renders it in a phone frame on demo data (`lib/member/demo*.ts`); dev-only, and Principal/HR in production.

**THE REVENUE APP (2026-10-05):** /member is `components/member/app/RevenueApp.tsx`, built to the Claude Design prototype (Revenue Mobile, kept outside the repo): Today / Insight / Quote / Arena / Profile, one URL state (`app/route.ts`), its own palette (`[data-revenue-app]` in globals.css), desktop by container query. Every rule number is VP's (bands, cost divisor, KPI), never the prototype's. The old `MemberWorkspace` survives only in `preview/`; `?app=proto` renders the new app on the prototype's own data, which is what it is pixel-compared against. On a phone the type is ONE STEP SMALLER than the prototype's (user 2026-10-06): every font-size is `calc(Npx * var(--rv-type))`, .92 on the phone's `.screen`, 1 on a desktop; `app/type-scale.test.ts` refuses a plain px.

**PULL TO REFRESH (2026-10-06):** nothing on screen changes on its own (user: 除非我 拉下来 refresh，它才会直接变) — the minute's background refresh is GONE. A pull from the page's top (`shell/PullRefresh.tsx`), or a return after 10 min away, bumps `gen`: every read runs again and KEEPS what is on screen until its answer lands (clears only when the person/month changes); keyed page reads go through `useKeyedRead`. The dot spins until all `track`ed reads land, the snapshot (`refreshMemberData`) last. Every build renames the actions, and the browser's own pull-reload is off on the app: a pull refused as `UnrecognizedActionError` (`isStaleAppError`) RELOADS the page — never over a sheet or unsaved brand weeks. While /member loads or cannot load, `loading.tsx` / `error.tsx` are the app's own frame (`app/shell/AppFrame.tsx`) + a spinner / *Try again* (2026-10-06).

**ONE REQUEST FOR A MOMENT'S READS (2026-10-06):** the client runs Server Actions ONE AT A TIME, so a pull's eight reads cost the SUM of their times (dev 11.7 s). `liveSource` now hands every read to `createReadBatcher` (`app/read-batch.ts`): reads asked in the same task leave as ONE action, `readMemberBatch` (`batch-actions.ts`, ≤16), which calls the very same read actions side by side — same checks, same session — so a pull costs its slowest read (dev 1.7 s) and a page opens in two requests, not 6–14. Writes and the quote's price still go straight.

**THE APP OPENS ON WHAT THE PHONE KEPT (2026-10-08, user: 每次进来好像都在 loading … 进去的时候先看旧的数据，等它 refresh 之后才是 新的数据):** every read's last answer is kept per ACCOUNT in IndexedDB (`app/read-memory.ts`, `createLiveSource(account)`); the next open shows those at once — `peek` in `useKeyedRead`, `useInsightData`, `useArenaData`, `useMonthArena` and the two-month book — and reads everything again behind them, the pull's dot spinning until the open's reads land. Nothing is asked before the phone's answers load (≤300 ms). /login forgets it all (every sign-out ends there), a week-old answer is dropped, the account is in every key; only what the reads already hand the browser — no page (sw.js still caches none), no cost. The snapshot every open waited for no longer carries the Quote's catalogue: `loadMemberData({ lite: true })` = `member_*_snapshot(p_full => false)` (mig `20261008160000`, overloads WITHOUT a default; the zero-arg calls are `(true)`): Principal 610 ms / 480 KB → 4 ms / 13 KB. The catalogue is its own read (`catalogue`, `loadMemberCatalogue`) when the Quote or its brand list opens, or once in the background when the phone kept none. Today's reads leave as ONE request: this month's block, KPI and Arena no longer wait for the two-month book to name the month being worked; last month's Arena is read only by brand weeks, requests and the team map. Loading is never a zero: Today's hero, Sales / Margin / KPI and the boards show a placeholder, and no place says "Not ranked yet" (Arena hero and hub too) — "RM 0k · No sales yet" while loading read exactly like a month with no sales (prod: income 2.6–8 s, Arena 3.3–8 s on every View-as open before). **My income** is Comm Cal's own engine run for the member's month (`loadMemberIncome`: the service role, only AFTER `member_income_context` authorised the session — the engine reads costs no member may; only their own money and bills leave the server): LIVE until HR closes them, then the close's own figures (user: 跟着 up to date … close 的时候就是 fix). A month before the cutover was paid from the claim form, outside the portal: it shows the portal's OWN working of it, as Comm Cal's month page does, headed "Claim form month · portal's working", salary "—" unless payroll kept one (user 2026-10-05: 显示 VP 自己算的; `lib/member/income.ts`). In View as, Today's income is the viewed person's own (`snapshotIncome`); a Principal viewing nobody sees "{Fair} commission" — the department's total, named as such (`loadDepartmentIncome`, the Principal's own session).

**THE APP IS THE LIVE MONTH (2026-10-06, user: "on live date, not showing last month" / "也开在当月"):** Today (hero, Sales / Margin / KPI, Your Arena), Insight, the Arena and My income open on the CURRENT month even while last month is still being closed — that one is in the date sheet, marked Live. The bell's news of a settled month opens that month. Honours, Team, brand weeks and the claim form still read the month being closed (`working`).

**A SALE'S MONTH IS ITS OWN (same day, user: by record it follows the date, never a close):** `member_order_facts` counts every bill in its `period_month` for good (mig `20261006130000`; it used to move a bill a close had not paid into the next open month). A close decides only when a bill is PAID: Comm Cal's carry (`lib/commission/carry.ts`, read inside `loadCommissionInput`) takes an earlier month's unpaid bill only once that month is closed for its salesperson. It used to take every bill no run released, so October's page counted all 357 September bills again (Fair RM 185,329 for RM 24,835; KAR JUN RM 17,568 for RM 5,674). A replayed run still shows the earlier bills it paid. **My honours** = every finish of a FINAL month — Sales / Margin / KPI total and by brand, Championship, and the weekly booth of the member's brand week; ties shared — from the Arena's own rankings (`lib/member/honours.ts`). A month the portal CLOSED is kept once (`member_honours`, service role, so no browser can put a place there) and forgotten — taken afresh on the next open — when a close or run of it reopens, a brand week of it changes, one of its bills or lines changes (a Recost too: Fair's pay ignores margin, so a Recost reopens nobody) or the cutover moves past it; a change of RULES (divisor, bands) keeps the closing ranks. A month BEFORE the cutover is NEVER kept (23HON): it is worked out afresh on every open, so HR's brand-week fixes and a Recost reach it as they reach the Arena (side review 2026-10-05 — a test had frozen Jun–Aug). **Requests** are numbered RQ-YYMM-NNN by trigger, with a history the member reads (`member_request_history`) and Cancel while pending with HR (23REQ). A claim is Outstation (spots per region × RM per spot, no invoice) or Airbnb (RM per person per NIGHT, invoice); the amount is worked out in the DB (`member_claim_detail`) from HR's rates (`member_claim_rates` / `_regions`), never taken from the phone; HR sets them on People › Member services › **Claim rates** (`MemberClaimRatesAdmin`, `member-admin/claim-rates-actions.ts`, rules in `lib/member/claim-rates-admin.ts` = the RPC's; gate as Brand weeks). The form reads them through `member_claim_settings()` — it called `member_claim_rates`, the TABLE, so until 2026-10-06 no claim could be sent; `lib/member/rpc-names.test.ts` now finds every RPC the member app calls among the migrations' functions. In View as the three forms open on the viewed person's choices and send nothing — *Read-only while viewing as* (user 2026-10-06: 只能看，不能 送出); only the member's own login sends.

**A CLAIM IS DECIDED ONCE (2026-10-08, user: 一个一个就好了):** HR approves or rejects a member's claim in People › Member services — never again on the portal's Claims page, which is POOL claims only now — and picks where the money comes from (`member_review_claim`, reached through `member_admin_action`): *Approve · add to payroll* = a `special_claim` pay line in the month the claim was SENT, or the next month still open for them, never before the sub-department's cutover (`member_claim_payroll_month`); the line is the claim's (`revenue_pay_lines.claim_id`) — Comm Cal's pencil shows it read-only and `revenue_save_pay_lines` neither edits nor deletes it. *Approve · request payment* = it waits on Finance's **Payments › Claim requests** (`claim_payment_queue`, which reads the RQ number Finance cannot), who raise the voucher (`claim_create_payment_voucher`: claim_payout, its pool, G&A 9000) for the Principal. A claim turns paid with its voucher or its payroll row (`claims_follow_their_voucher` / `_payroll`); a voided voucher sends it back to Finance's queue. The request says where (`payout` on `member_request_json`, `lib/member/claim-payout.ts`). Mig `20261008100000`.

**A CLAIM IS FOR YOUR BRAND WEEK (2026-10-06, user):** the phone sends only the weekend; the fair is the booth the Arena scores the member at (their pick, else auto-located by most bills — `member_claim_brand_week`, the same boards `claimWeeks` reads), its region the one HR set for the VENUE (`member_claim_venue_regions`; Claim rates lists only venues an ERP fair names — `source = 'erp'` — not the hand-typed June–August calendar's other spellings; unmapped = cannot be claimed), the amount `member_claim_detail_for`'s. A region ticked *Plane ticket* (Sabah, Sarawak) is the ticket's typed price + its invoice, no cap. **Airbnb is by head count:** up to `airbnb_group_max_pax` (HR, 3 to start) the member keys the INVOICE TOTAL and is paid it up to `airbnb_group_cap_per_night` × nights (user: 3 / RM 220 / 2 nights / invoice RM 500 → RM 440, for 1, 2 or 3 people alike); more people = RM 65 × pax × nights, nothing typed; no cap = everybody per person (`airbnbGroup` in `lib/member/claim-rules.ts`). Airbnb is for an OUTSTATION fair only — the venue's region has spots or is flown to (`isOutstation`); a local one (KL, 0 spots) has none — and a stay is at most 7 nights that covers the fair's days: in on or before its last day, out after its first (`stayProblem`; user 2026-10-06). The form opens on the newest brand week, months on the badges. Migs `20261006050000` / `20261006150000` / `20261006160000`. **My brand weeks** (Profile chip / Today's reminder; no prototype page) = this month's weekends then the month still worked, one brand per week, closed weeks locked for the member; a Principal viewing is the back office (any week). The **Commission scheme** chip opens the member's title, rank, rate and each brand's ladder — which parts a month PAYS is My income's to say. Memos, Team & capacity and View as (the Principal reads any member, read-only) read real data. Migs `20261005030000` / `040000` / `050000` / `060000` / `070000`.

**MEMBER SERVICES IN THE REVAMP (2026-10-10, HR revamp 4a/8; DESIGN.md v3.23):** `/member-admin` is its own page now — `member-admin/_components/MemberServicesView` (the kit's head and compact tabs: **Request reviews · Brand weeks · Claim rates · Publish memo**; Capacity went to Rules in PR2, **Product options** — only a pointer to Modular since the Product List — is gone). `components/member/MemberAdmin` is ONLY the member app's admin preview (`/member/preview`, demo data, its own look); its portal mode, `MemberBrandWeeksAdmin` and `MemberClaimRatesAdmin` were deleted. The page reads with itself, in parallel: `member_admin_snapshot()` (its Principal-only `products` — 452 KB — are dropped before the browser), `member_admin_request_facts()` and `member_memo_reads()` (mig `20261010160000`, DEFINER, gates `member_requests_manage` / `member_memos_manage`) and the claim rates (3 ms). **Tabs are the page's own state, not the URL**: Brand weeks reads its month (`fetchBrandWeeksAdmin`, ~280 ms) when it is FIRST opened — a user's click — and a URL pushed while an action is in flight is what strands the router (comm-cal.md › "Back while an action is in flight"); an opened tab stays mounted, hidden, so a draft survives switching. `?request=` still opens one request. **Request reviews**: the 360px list (All · Claim · Replacement · Waiting list; "Kind · RQ-…", the status chip) beside the open request — its facts named (`lib/member/member-services.ts`), the step, the note, the buttons, the audit history with who moved it. A waiting-list **Accept** needs no note (`ACCEPT_NOTE` goes on the record; `member_admin_action('qualify')` still wants one); Request more information and Reject ask for one first. A claim is decided once, as before (payroll / payment request, View invoice). A replacement keeps today's approve → link the successor → confirm steps in the new look until PR4b replaces them with Replaced by + effective date + the automatic run. **Brand weeks**: one chip per member-week in the brand's colours; a click cycles Akemi → Dunlopillo → Zanotti → Ergotex → not picked; a changed chip is peach and one bar saves them (`saveBrandWeeksAdmin`); a week nobody picked shows the board's brand DASHED; what is stored and every booth's bills are the chip's tooltip, the member's login / last save the name's. **Claim rates**: the two rate cards (Base is a choice of the region names — the stored base kept if it is none of them), the regions table (Flown to as a box), the venues with their regions; ONE Save at the top, "Saved" while nothing differs. **Publish memo**: Title, Body, Audience — sent from People & Culture, in force today, acknowledged, version 1 — and each published memo "read by N".

**THE WAITING LIST HOLDS NO PLACES (2026-10-10, HR revamp 3b/8):** an accepted applicant waits on Sales Groups › Waiting list until HR transfers them (People Management's employee form) or fails them — nothing is reserved automatically any more (`member_reserve_waiting` reserves nothing, mig `20261010140000`; `comm-cal.md`). Two statuses are new: `failed` (taken off the list — the app says *Not placed* / *Removed from the waiting list*, red; a failed request is not offered as a replacement's waiting-list source) and `scheduled` (a replacement approved for a later date — Member services, PR4). A transfer leaves the request `executed` with `allocated_staff_id` = the new employee and the note "Transferred to People Management as HVnnn". Member services' waiting-list notice now points to Sales Groups (*Open the waiting list →*) instead of promising a reserved place. The waiting-list FORM is versioned (`member_waiting_form()` lets in `has_perm('member_requests')` too, for the app); the app's request form keeps its four fixed questions until PR7 reads the version, offers the application form and uploads the signed one.

**CAPACITY LEFT MEMBER SERVICES (2026-10-10, the HR revamp):** a sub-department's headcount limit is its own rule now — Revenue › Rules › **Capacity** (`comm-cal.md`) — so People › Member services lost its *Capacity & waiting list* tab and its *Match vacancies* button; the hourly `/api/member-queue` sweep still reserves a free seat for an eligible applicant until PR3 replaces the waiting list. Waiting-list requests are still reviewed on *Request reviews*. `/member-admin?request=<id>` opens one request (Sales Groups' Waiting list and Replacement list send HR there to decide it).

**INSIGHT (2026-09-16)** answers "how am I doing" from COMMITTED ORDERS, not from the month's statement — a run happens once a month, so the statement is last month's answer for most of it. Three views: My sales (own revenue, KPI units, the gap to the next rung's gate, blended margin, every order in its margin band), Ranking (everybody, split by sub-department) and Downline (each leg of the caller's team). It counts the way `engine.ts` counts: commissionable lines only, the DEPOSIT GATE applied (held revenue reported separately, never inside sales), carry-forward honoured via `comm_order_releases`, and an order with no usable margin counted in sales but left out of the blended margin. SALES AND MARGIN ONLY — no commission, rate or salary of anybody, including the caller's own on a board. Gated by the `member_insight` permission, which the neutral member allowlist in `current_permissions()` had to learn (mig 20260916010000). Arithmetic in `lib/member/insight.ts`, data in `member_insight_snapshot()`.

**TWO MARGINS, AND THEY ONLY SEE THE SECOND (2026-09-19):** layer 1 is price vs the ERP's cost — what the COMPANY makes; layer 2 is price vs `cost ÷ comm_schemes.revenue_cost_divisor` (Fair 0.7) — what the SALESPERSON is measured and paid on. Layer 2 is the only one the member app may show (user 2026-09-19). `member_order_facts` is the ONLY member-side function that touches `cost_amount`, and RLS keeps `sales_orders`/`_lines` to hr+admin, so that one division is the whole boundary — it had been missing since PR #114 and the app read 12–22 points high (mig `20260919050000`).

**AND NEVER HOW (2026-10-05):** no screen says what that cost is made of — no "team cost", no "cost ÷ 0.7", no ratio (user: wording like that leaks our info; `components/member/cost-basis-wording.test.ts` fails on such a line) — and the ratio never reaches a browser, not even through DevTools: `member_quote_context` (a member's own login can call it) has bands + KPI items only, the cost rules come from `member_quote_cost_rules` (service role only, after the context authorised the session), `QuoteResult` / `MemberOrderBook` / `member_order_book` carry no `costDivisor`. Migs `20261005110000` / `120000`.

**MARGIN INCENTIVE (2026-09-19):** the Arena's fourth board, and NOT a ranking — the tier is money and everyone on a tier gets the same money, so a board of tiers would be a board of pay. It is the viewed person's own: tier, RM, each brand's margin, and how many points short the BINDING brand is. `arenaIncentive()` in `lib/member/arena.ts` mirrors `marginIncentiveAcrossBrands` — thresholds follow the BRAND and differ (AKEMI/ERGOTEX 36+43, DUNLOP/ZANOTTI 33+36, mig `20260919040000`), EVERY brand sold must clear a tier, earned tiers are the INTERSECTION and the money stacks; a brand with an uncosted bill, or a bill with no brand, HOLDS the lot and the board names it. The ladder rides in the snapshot (`tiers`, mig `20260919060000`).

**FAIR RESULTS (2026-09-21):** the Arena's *Championship & Fair results* page said "not connected yet" because a finish needs a fair's venue and DATES, and an order date beside a branch label cannot identify one. `20260920050000` gave `comm_venue_events` start/end and stamps every bill with `sales_orders.fair_event_id`, so the board is now real: each completed fair ranked on its own, medal counts per person, and the **Highest Fair Record** (largest single fair, all-time, outside any window). PROVISIONAL AND IT SAYS SO — a recorded sale is not attendance, so somebody who worked a fair and sold nothing cannot be told from somebody who was never there; and a bill no fair could claim is COUNTED OUT LOUD (`unassignedOrders`/`Sales`, 196 bills / RM 1.12M on 2026-09-21) rather than spread over the fairs that did happen. Nothing is awarded: every `points` field is 0 and the championship stays `pending`. Same roster, committed sources, deposit gate and commissionable lines as `member_arena_for_staff` — a fair's sales must agree with the month's. `lib/member/arena-history.ts`, mig `20260921010000`, and the design-preview `ArenaFairResults`/`RankingList` are the live board (parameterized `selfId`, so one component serves both).

**BOOTH BY BOOTH (2026-10-03):** a result is a brand's BOOTH at a fair, not the fair (the month page's Sales exhibition), so every booth is ranked on its own (small ones too — 人少也算) and holds EVERY bill written there (no-pick bills at the seller's booth, ten brandless old ones on the fair's no-brand board): the boards add up to the old whole-fair totals. A seller is RANKED on every board they sold at (a 30k that is third there shows third — 依然会显示属于那个名次); ONE A WEEK EARNS POINTS (`scoring`, user: 你照样上榜，只是你 上榜了没有拿到积分 … 能算积分的只有一个): the booth of the brand they pick for that week in *My brand weeks* — one dropdown per fair weekend (the week a fair opens in, named by its Friday: Sep 2026 = 4/11/18/25), stored as `member_arena_brand_weeks.picks` — at the fair with the most bills for it. On the others the place stands — sellers behind it move down — and reads *No points* (`offWeekOrders`/`Sales`); the medal counts and Highest Fair Record read scoring boards only. A WEEK CLOSES seven days after its fair's last day (`member_arena_week_closes_on`; 5–7 → open to the 14th; user: 结束之后的一个星期 … 没有填就 closing 了): until then a week nobody picked shows *Unconfirmed*, after it *Auto-located* — the brand they opened the MOST BILLS for that week (开最多单), then sales — and the member's save refuses to change it. After that only the ERP (the bills' booth picks) or the BACK OFFICE changes it: the Principal's view of a member makes *Member brand weeks* an editor for every week (`member_principal_save_brand_week_picks`), and HR has **People › Member services › Brand weeks** — a department's month on one grid, member × week, what is stored and the booths written at under each, one Save (`member_admin_brand_weeks` / `member_admin_save_brand_weeks`, gate `member_is_manager('member_requests_manage')`, mig `20261003210000`; every save records `updated_by`). On 2026-10-03 no Revenue seller has a member login, so every finish starts guessed. A bill with no ERP booth pick counts at its seller's booth. Highest Fair Record = the best single booth. `member_arena_booth_history` (+ principal twin), migs `20261003180000` (additive — the old `member_arena_history*` stay unread), `20261003190000` (the lock) and `20261003200000` (every booth shows, one scores), all LIVE before the code merged. **TEAM** is a mapping map, not an indented list: every sub-department as a frame, its directors above it, one card per MEMBERSHIP, drag to pan. Placed by the same `layoutTree` the portal's charts use, via `lib/member/org-map-layout.ts`, on `member_org_map()`. Names, titles and reporting lines only.
