**ROWS FROM ANOTHER MONTH (2026-10-12, the user on a salesperson's ledger: "if the commission is pay in current month, but the bill is come from another month, i need the bar show different colour … then add a column status (show the reason — top up deposit, drop sales)"; and "for drop and up sales, that effect the commision need to show up in the table list as well"):** a ledger's first column after the bill is **Status** (`lib/commission/row-origin.ts`, spec §4.19): *Top up deposit* (an earlier month's bill its month's close held for the deposit — `comm_salesperson_closes.deferred_orders`), *Late key-in* (an earlier month's bill its close did not have, or a bill of this month sold at an earlier month's fair — `saleMonthOf` in `lib/fair-calendar.ts`, the TS twin of `sales_order_sale_month()`, from `raw.fair.fairDate`, else the fair's month), *Early key-in*, *Upsell* / *Drop sales* (with the month of the bill they changed), *Below deposit* (carried, still held — not paid here) and *After close* (this month's bill its salesperson's close did not take — the next open month pays it). Every way out of `ingest_erp_sales_order` goes through `erp_sync_after_delivery` (production's body with four swaps, mig `20261013010000`, md5 `e114d662…` → `400c57fc…`); a fault in the trail is a `change_trail_failed` warning on the delivery, never its failure. A change is paid only in a month AFTER the one that gave the commission (`comm_upsales_after_settled`, 23ADJ; the Pay button is shut and says from when) — that month already counts the bill as the ERP has it. A pending question's `period_month` is the first month it may be paid in.
