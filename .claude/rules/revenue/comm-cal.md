**THE FAIR SETS THE MONTH (2026-09-20):** `period_month` followed `header.so_date` — the day the ERP document was OPENED IN THE BACK OFFICE, not the day the customer signed at the fair. Of August's 280 matchable bills only FIVE carried the right date; median lag +4 days, worst +42. Mid-month that is harmless; at month end it moves a whole weekend into the next payroll — Kuching (8/28-31), MITC and IOI GECC, **RM 674,744 of August landing in September**, while July's month-end fairs sat in August. The two cancel to a 10% difference in the total, which is why nobody saw that half the month's bills had been swapped. THE ERP CANNOT HELP: every `header` key of all 552 deliveries was enumerated 2026-09-20 — no fair / event / campaign field exists and `venue_id` is NULL on every order including the ones that arrived that morning. So the calendar is the portal's own fact. `comm_venue_events` BECOMES THE FAIR: `start_date` / `end_date`, and the old `UNIQUE(venue, month)` drops — one hall holds two or three fairs a month with a DIFFERENT organiser each (REX at MVEC SOUTHKEY mid-August, BIGHOME at the same hall month end), which is the ALTER 20260918040000 said it was leaving room for. An EXCLUDE forbids two fairs at one place overlapping; a partial UNIQUE keeps one undated row per (place, month). `venue_event_for_order()` is the ONE rule — the fair open on the order date, else the most recent one there that closed within 45 days (worst observed lag 42) — and it stamps `sales_orders.fair_event_id` / `fair_source` by TRIGGER, the way `sales_orders_ignored_agent` does and for the same reason. Editing a fair re-stamps that place's bills. TWO STEPS, ALWAYS: saving a fair MOVES NOTHING. `revenue_fair_calendar_moves` shows every bill that would move; `revenue_apply_fair_calendar` moves `period_month` AND `batch_id` together (the run reads orders by BATCH) and REFUSES a month whose run has paid (23RUN), like `recost_sales_order_lines`. Every run over a touched month must be computed again. `event_date` is NOT touched: spec 4.4 reads Open Bill as "1st/2nd/3rd bill of the EVENT DAY", and collapsing a four-day fair onto one date would turn three bonuses into one — a separate decision with its own money attached. UI was *Fair calendar* + *Still to place* on Revenue > Venues until 2026-09-27 (the ERP names the fair now — see above). `lib/fair-calendar.ts`, `fair-actions.ts`, mig `20260920050000`.

**CONFIRMED MOVES (2026-09-23):** while next month's fairs are untyped the 45-day grace reaches BACK across the month end — of 174 bills waiting to leave September, August's claim form named 96; the other 78 were September's own fairs at the same halls, and no grace width splits them (lags overlap). A bill a HUMAN placed (`fair_source = 'manual'`, e.g. pinned from the month's claim form) is `confirmed`, and `revenue_apply_fair_calendar(sub,p_confirmed_only)` — *Move the N confirmed* — moves only those. Never pin a guess to "no fair": a pin is forever and would stop it finding its fair once that is typed. Mig `20260923010000`.

**A RE-SEND KEEPS THE MONTH (2026-09-24):** the ERP re-sends a document on every save and `ingest_erp_sales_order` re-derived the month from `so_date` each time, so every calendar move lasted until the bill's next save (4 went back overnight; 221, RM 1.36M, were queued behind them). **COSTING SETTING + KPI ITEMS (2026-09-27; KPI items on RULES since 2026-10-08):** Costing Setting is a Product List tab, per sub-department (`?sub=`) = the ratio a cost is divided by for the Revenue Team (`comm_schemes.revenue_cost_divisor`, Fair 0.7) and SKUs with a ratio of their OWN (`comm_cost_ratios`, e.g. ÷ 0.6), applied per line in the engine (`lib/commission/cost-basis.ts`), the month page, the member facts and the calculator. KPI items = a SKU or a bed frame / sofa SPECIAL (`comm_kpi_items.source` 'sku' | 'option', `option_pool` / `option_value`) with one amount.
