# HR tab — HR's view (the shell) and the revamp's kit

HR's view of the portal — the hr role, and the Principal viewing as HR — and the `components/hrv` kit its revamped pages are drawn with. The pages themselves are in their tabs' files (`../revenue/comm-cal.md`, `people.md`, `../revenue/revenue-app.md`). DESIGN.md › "The HR revamp" is the visual spec.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab). New decisions are added here, dated.

**HR'S OWN SHELL (2026-10-10, the Claude Design handoff *Sidebar design revamp from HR*: `.design/hr-revamp/handoff`, README = spec, `design/Revenue App.dc.html` = source of truth, variant A; user: "colour tone, motion, function & button 100% same with prototype"):** `lib/hr-view.ts` `isHrView(role)` decides; PortalFrame draws `.hrv-shell` — `HrSidebar` (square, full height, 248 ↔ 64px content-box, five toned sections from `hr-nav.ts`: Overview · HR & Payroll · Directors · Revenue · People · Admin; one pill slides to the clicked row, tile swaps tones + scale 1.06, label nudges 2px; the H tile folds it, cookie `vp-hr-nav`), `HrTopBar` (white; the "HR view" words are the view switcher for an account that may switch; no theme toggle — the prototype has none and the shell is drawn light), and the sheet as `<main>` scrolling inside `.hrv-frame` (`data-hrv-overlay`: drawers, windows and the toast portal there, covering the sheet only). Every other view keeps its shell (user: the design is HR's view). HR's sidebar drops Claims and Revenue › Department (pages kept). Phone: the old TopBar / tab bar / More sheet. Tests: `hr-nav.test.ts`, `PortalFrame.fin.test.tsx`, `layout.test.tsx`.

**ROUND 2 — THE KIT'S ADDITIONS (2026-10-11, the handoff's second package `.design/hr-r2/handoff`; plan `.design/hr-r2/PLAN.md`; DESIGN.md v3.30):** `components/hrv/select.tsx` `Select` = the soft dropdown (README 18.1, `dropdown.js`): a real `<select>` drawn radius 10 (12 on the guest page) with the chevron, whose pointer press opens our menu instead of the browser's — a pick sets the value the browser's way and fires `change`, so onChange hears it; Esc closes only the menu. Every `<select>` on a round-2 page is a `Select` (round 1's pages are Part 1's to switch). Drawers slide in (`hvDrawerIn .34s`), scrims fade `.25s`, windows `.2s` — in the kit, so every drawer moves.

**THE BRAND HAS MORE AIR (2026-10-11, round 2's README 3 — "Part 1"):** `HrSidebar`'s brand block pads `20px 12px 26px 14px` and has no rule under it (Overview starts 84px down, as the prototype's). Measured equal.

**EVERY SELECT ON HR'S PAGES IS THE SOFT DROPDOWN (2026-10-11, round 2's Part 1 — README 18.1 "every `<select>`"; the user: "Proceed"; DESIGN.md v3.42):** the pages HR's sidebar opens (round 1's pages, HR Costing, the month's workbench and its bills, People, Member services, Claims, Audit Log) and the top bar's view switch use `Select`; `soft-select-coverage.test.ts` parses them and refuses a bare `<select>` (a comment that mentions one is not one). Three ways to draw one: the kit's field (what `className={hv.select}` drew — drop that class), `bare` (the page's own box, the soft field's radius / chevron / ring over it), `plain` (the field exactly as drawn, only the menu swapped — for what the prototype draws as something else: the Members grid's 6px boxes with a ▾, Scheme rules' quiet values, Brand weeks' chip, the "HR view" words). The menu shows an `<optgroup>`'s label over its options (dropdown.js flattened them). The focus ring is `!important` so the portal's .12 glow doesn't cover the prototype's .18. **A popover that closes on a press or a scroll outside itself must ask `inSoftMenu(event.target)` first** — the menu is portaled to `<body>`, and without it the bill menu closed under its own Share list before the pick landed (`BillContextMenu.test.tsx`). Finance's and the Principal's own pages keep the browser's select (not in the handoff).

**THE SOFT DROPDOWN ON A TOUCH SCREEN (2026-10-11, the user, from a phone: the Revenue app's *Book a room* would not open Department or From / To; DESIGN.md v3.58):** a list of more than eight has a search box, and the menu focused it on opening — on a phone that raised the keyboard, the keyboard resized the window, and a resize closed the menu at once (Room, four options, opened fine). Every `Select` on every phone surface had it (the Revenue app, the HR app, the guest page). Now, where `(pointer: coarse)`: the search box waits for a tap (no focus on opening); a resize or a scroll — the keyboard coming or going, the address bar — re-places the menu with its field inside the visual viewport instead of closing it, and closes it only once the field has left the screen. With a mouse nothing changed (the box takes the keys at once; a resize or a scroll closes). Tests `components/hrv/select.test.tsx` › on a touch screen; production showed the old path (search focused, a resize → shut) in a touch emulation.

**THE KIT (2026-10-10):** `components/hrv/` — `kit.tsx` (PageHead, Cards/Card, SectHead, Chip, Toggle, GridTable/GridHead/GridRow, HistoryEvent, Facts, SecLabel, `btn()`), `tabs.tsx` (Tabs, `useTabParam` — `?tab=` pushed with history), `overlay.tsx` (Drawer, Window, `useEscape` — Esc closes the top one), `toast.tsx` (`flash()` + one ToastHost per shell), `icons.tsx`, `hrv.module.css`. Colours only through `--hv-*` (globals.css, on `.sheet-theme` and `.hrv-overlay`). A revamped page undoes what Tailwind's preflight moves: `line-height: normal`, and `box-sizing: content-box` where the prototype sizes a box with padding / border. Cross-check every page with a measuring script (prototype vs live, element by element: computed font / colour / size / padding / border / radius / background) — the PR1 run left only intended tag differences (real `<button>` / `<a>` where the prototype has spans).

**THE SIDEBAR IS SHARED AND 212PX (2026-10-14, the Finance revamp v2 — `.design/fin-r2/handoff`, whose README draws both views on "HrSidebar (212px wide, 64 folded)" and the HR Overview "sidebar 212px with hr-nav.ts sections"; DESIGN.md v4.3):** `HrSidebar` is now HR's sections over the shared `ViewSidebar` (the pill, the fold, the warming, the footer), which Finance's view draws with its own categories (`FinSidebar`, `fin-nav.ts`, `../finance/finance-view.md` › FINANCE'S OWN SHELL). Both are 212px content-box (248 before), 64 folded, on the one fold cookie `vp-hr-nav`. The rail is wrapped (`.rail`) so a footer menu can sit over it; HR's footer stays words, Finance's is the account menu's button. A row's page owned by another list (`NavOwner`) lights that list in both. **The sheet sits right under the greeting bar** on a computer, in both views: `.hrv-frame` has no top padding from 768px up (the prototypes draw the sheet `margin: 0 10px 10px` under the bar), and the kit's drawers and scrims start at the frame's top to match (`hrv.module.css`).
