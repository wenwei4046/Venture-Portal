# HR tab — Meeting Rooms and the Guest Link

Admin › Meeting Rooms, `/admin/rooms` — `src/app/(portal)/admin/rooms/`, `src/lib/rooms/`; Admin › Guest Link, `/admin/guest-link`, and the public `/g/<code>` — `src/app/(portal)/admin/guest-link/`, `src/app/(guest)/g/`, `src/lib/guest/`. DESIGN.md › "Meeting Rooms" and "Guest link" are the visual spec; how a page and its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

**MEETING ROOMS (2026-10-11, the HR revamp's second package — README §14 of `.design/hr-r2/handoff`, plan `.design/hr-r2/PLAN.md`; mig `20261011090000`).** The user: Meeting Rooms in EVERY view (HR's sidebar Admin above Audit Log; the old shell's Reports & Admin above Audit Log; `PAGE_TABS` hr / fin / pri / dept `.meeting_rooms`); the rooms START EMPTY (HR adds them on the Rooms tab); **whoever booked a meeting is the one who changes or cancels it** — the shared accounts are going, everyone gets their own login. Tabs Schedule · Rooms, Rooms only for `rooms.edit` (key `meeting_rooms_manage`).

- **Tables** `meeting_rooms`, `meeting_room_bookings` (ref `BK-301`… from `meeting_room_booking_ref_seq`; minutes 480–1200 in half hours; `source` portal / app / hr_app / guest; an EXCLUSION constraint stops two live bookings of a room overlapping — the database's last word on a clash, worded *Someone just took that slot*) and `meeting_room_booking_events` (the drawer's audit trail, written by the functions; `audit_register` mode none). RLS on, NO policy, nothing granted — every read and write is one of the functions below.
- **Who may.** Read: `rooms.view`, or `revenue_app.view:own` (a member reading from the Revenue app). Book: `rooms.create`, or `revenue_app.view:own` with source `app` — a member books for themselves only. Rooms: `rooms.edit`. A booking is changed or cancelled only by whoever booked it (`booked_by_user = auth.uid()`), and a guest-link booking also by a holder of `rooms.edit` (Meeting rooms · Edit). A booking made in the portal or an app is never the guest link's to touch.
- **Functions** (DEFINER, `has_any_action`): readers `meeting_rooms_range(from, to)` (at most 62 days: the bookable rooms and their live bookings, each with `mine` / `canChange`), `meeting_rooms_booking(id)`, `meeting_rooms_rooms()` (with bookings ahead), `meeting_rooms_people()` (who is asking, who may be picked, departments with somebody working, sub-departments) — all four on `READS_WHILE_VIEWING_AS`; writers `meeting_rooms_book(…, p_source, p_sub_department)` (new or edit; checks the room, the half hours, the clash, the seats), `meeting_rooms_cancel(id)`, `meeting_rooms_save_room`, `meeting_rooms_delete_room` (soft, refused while the room has bookings ahead — turn Bookable off instead).
- **Other callers** (the Revenue app, the HR app) use `lib/rooms/read.ts` (server-only readers, a date RANGE) and `lib/rooms/actions.ts` (`bookRoom` with its `source`, `cancelBooking`); parsing and wording are `lib/rooms/model.ts`.
- **The day is the address's** (`?day=`), written with `history.replaceState(null, …)` — never `history.state`, which Next's router does not read: a Server Action posts to the address the router knows, so a booking's refresh rendered today and threw the booked day away (found in the E2E, 2026-10-11). A refreshed page is taken only when it is for the day the address asks for.
- **Tests:** `lib/rooms/model.test.ts`, `admin/rooms/_components/book-form.test.ts`, PGlite `tests/member-sql/validate-meeting-rooms.mjs`. An E2E on prod names its rows "E2E …" and deletes them after, with their `audit_events`.

**THE GUEST LINK (2026-10-11, README §15–16; mig `20261011091000`).** Admin › Guest Link `/admin/guest-link` (HR's view: `PAGE_TABS` `hr.guest_link`; Guest link · View reads, Edit changes) and the PUBLIC page `/g/<code>` — its own route group `(guest)`, no portal layout, no `requirePage`, no sign-in. The user: the link starts OFF; HQ's meetings are never a guest's to change ("can set hq de 不给 guest edit 就好"); a meeting booked through the link may be changed or cancelled by any guest, who gives their name for the trail.

- **The code is the only key.** `guest_link` (one row: on, `hv-` + 8 of 32 letters and digits — about 10^12, not guessable; the prototype's 4 would be), the parts (`booking`, `hiring`), `days_ahead` 1–21. New link makes a new code and the old one reads closed at once; so does any wrong code — the page never says more than "This link is closed".
- **The guest page reads and writes as a stranger** (`lib/guest/client.ts`: the publishable key, no session cookie — a signed-in portal user is the same stranger there), through four DEFINER functions granted to anon BY NAME: `guest_link_page(code)`, `guest_link_day(code, day)`, `guest_link_book(code, …, actor)`, `guest_link_cancel(code, id, actor)`. Each checks the code (`guest_link_open`: on, current, the part open); book and cancel touch only `source = 'guest'` bookings; a guest books only a room switched on under *Rooms guests can book*, inside the days ahead, within its seats, on a free slot. Never the service role.
- **The subsidiaries are the link's own list** (`guest_link_subsidiaries`: code up to 3, name, departments), seeded ONCE from Finance's active `companies` (codes = their short codes cut to three: 2990 → 299, The Cont → CON — HR edits them); `companies` is never written. A company with no departments asks none. A booking keeps the company and department as picked; its code follows the subsidiary (a code change rewrites `guest_code`). The Meeting Rooms legend reads the codes (`guest_link_legend()`).
- **Admin functions:** `guest_link_admin()` (the page; its upcoming guest bookings as `meeting_rooms_rows` reads them, so Open is the Meeting Rooms drawer — Meeting rooms · Edit cancels or changes them), `guest_link_set(on, booking, hiring, days)`, `guest_link_new_code()`, `guest_link_room(room, on)`, `guest_link_save_subsidiary(…)`, `guest_link_remove_subsidiary(id)` (soft). Writes in `lib/guest/admin-actions.ts` (`requireAction('guest_link', 'edit')`), the guest page's in `lib/guest/actions.ts` (no gate to ask: the database checks the code).
- **Request manpower** (mig `20261011092000`) opens once the Hiring module's guest door is there (looked up by name, so it never fails before): `guest_link_page` hands `hiring_guest_form()` — Hiring › Form settings — and `guest_link_hire(code, subsidiary, name, answers, path, file)` checks the code (on, the part open), the company on the link's list, the name, an attachment under guest/<CURRENT code>/ that is really in storage, then calls `hiring_file_guest_request` (Hiring's own door, service-role only otherwise). The request lands in Hiring › Requests as "Submitted via guest link"; its number comes back to the guest. **Files:** the guest's browser uploads to the private `hiring-files` bucket as a stranger (`lib/guest/upload.ts`, no session) — the storage policy `guest_link_uploads` lets anon / authenticated INSERT under guest/<code>/ only while `guest_link_may_upload(code)` (anon by name) says the part is open; nothing reads it back but Hiring. The form follows the prototype's four steps + Check and send (`HireFlow.tsx`, `lib/guest/model.ts` `hireGroups`): ROLE ids and every other short field → The role, age / gender / race → Candidate requirements, long text + the file → Job description; five or fewer choices are pills. "Send to HR" is never pressed in an E2E on prod — it writes into Hiring's table.
- **Tests:** `lib/guest/model.test.ts`, `GuestPage.test.tsx` (HQ's meeting has no buttons; a cancel asks the name), PGlite `tests/member-sql/validate-guest-link.mjs` (both migrations verbatim, as anon). An E2E turns the link on only for the test and off after, names its rows "E2E …" and deletes them with their `audit_events`.

**THE GUEST LINK, HARDENED (2026-10-11, after the round-2 security review `.design/hr-r2/review-security.md` — M1, N3, N4, L8, L9; mig `20261011120000`).** The link stays OFF on production; nothing here turned it on. The user's rules hold as before: a guest edits or cancels only guest-link bookings, never the portal's or an app's; no service role; Finance's `companies` is never written; every guest write is a DEFINER function that checks the code, granted to anon by name.

- **One upload name.** `guest_link_uploads` takes an INSERT into `hiring-files` only for `guest/hv-<8 of A–Z without I and O, 2–9>/<uuid>.<pdf|doc|docx|png|jpg|jpeg|webp>` — exactly what `lib/guest/upload.ts` writes (lower-case uuid and extension) — AND `guest_link_may_upload(<the code>)` still says yes. Before, any name went in under `guest/<code>/`; only the request checked the shape.
- **Hourly caps**, inside the functions, counted from the tables' own timestamps over the last hour (`now()`, an instant — not the office's day): `guest_link_may_upload` says no once 40 files went under `guest/` (`storage.objects.created_at`); `guest_link_hire` refuses once 20 guest requests were filed (`hiring_requests.submitted_at`); `guest_link_book` once 40 bookings or changes came in through the link (its trail lines *Booked via guest link* / *Edited via guest link* — a change writes as much as a booking); `guest_link_cancel` once 40 cancellations did (*Cancelled via guest link*). Their words: *Too many requests from the guest link just now. Try again later.* A cap counts the whole link, not one guest: honest use (a few bookings a day from the subsidiaries) never meets one, and a leaked code buys an hour's worth before it waits — HR can still press New link. Production's bodies, one statement swapped in each (`pg_temp.swap_check`); grants and owners kept.
- **Request manpower starts OFF** (`guest_link.hiring` DEFAULT false, the one row set false): turning the link on opens room booking only, and the anonymous form and its uploads open when HR also switches Request manpower on (Admin › Guest Link) — the user's call to flip.
- **A stranger reads only refusals** (`lib/guest/model.ts` `guestRefusal`, used by every call in `lib/guest/actions.ts`): a P0001 / 42501 / 22023 message passes on as the database wrote it, a clash (23P01) is *Someone just took that slot. Pick another time.*, and anything else — a constraint's name, a type error, Postgres's own *permission denied for function …*, a network failure — becomes the call's own fallback sentence.
- **Headers** (`next.config.ts`, `/g/:path*`): `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` (the address holds the code), `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'`, `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- **Still open from the review:** removing unattached `guest/` uploads (L7 — a DELETE policy for Hiring · Edit, or a cron path, which would be a new service-role caller: the user's decision) and how much of HQ's meetings the schedule shows a guest (L1, `questions-guest.md` Q3).
- **Tests:** PGlite `validate-guest-link.mjs` (the pinned name, each cap at its edge and again an hour later, the default and the row; the migration run twice swaps once), `lib/guest/actions.test.ts` (each call's refusals and fallbacks), `model.test.ts` (`guestRefusal`), `lib/guest/page-headers.test.ts`.

**MEETING ROOMS AFTER THE USER'S ANSWERS (2026-10-11, mig `20261011150000` + `150100`; questions in `.design/hr-r2/QUESTIONS.md`, review `.design/hr-r2/review-security.md`).**

- **Booked for somebody else (L3):** `meeting_room_bookings.on_behalf_by` keeps the account's name when *Booked by* names somebody else (a new booking; a change that names somebody else again sets it anew, one that keeps the person keeps it, naming oneself clears it). **WHO A MEETING TELLS (2026-10-11, mig `20261011180000`, `notify_meeting_booking`).** A booking tells the person it is for (*Grace Lim booked a room for you*) and each attendee with a login (*… added you to a meeting*); a change tells whoever is still in it (*Meeting changed · title*), whoever was added and whoever was taken off; a cancel tells everyone in it (*Meeting cancelled · title*, "by …"). Never whoever did it. A guest-link booking made, changed or cancelled tells the holders of the `rooms.guest` topic (Meeting rooms · Edit, kept by the HR app). Rules: `.claude/rules/portal-wide/pwa.md` › PHONE NOTIFICATIONS.

**PICK A DAY, SEE THE HALF HOUR (2026-10-11, the user; DESIGN.md v3.51).** *"这个点开的话，它可以直接选那个月份的日期"* — the pager's label is a button that opens a month card (`_components/DayPicker.tsx`): six weeks Monday first (`monthWeeks`, as Repayment Schedule's calendar), the asked day dark, today ringed; ‹ › on the card turn only its month, a day pressed goes there through the same `onDay` as ‹ › (so `?day=` and the dropped-late-read rule hold), the day shown / Escape / a press outside close it and give the label back its focus; arrow keys move a day or a week. ‹ › on the pager still step one day. *"当我的 mouse pointer 指到那个 bar 的时候，它是有 effect 的"* — every FREE half hour of a room (`freeHalfHours`) is drawn as an aria-hidden `[data-time]` span that lights on `:hover` (only `@media (hover: hover)`, so a phone's tap leaves nothing lit) with `+ 09:30`; the press stays the column's. Because the bar under the pointer is what you press, `slotAt` now stops at 19:30, not 19:00, and `blankForm` makes a meeting an hour long but never past 20:00 — the HR app's rule (`hourFrom`). **A day already past** (shown day < the office's today) neither lights nor opens Book: `meeting_rooms_book` refuses it (*Pick today or a day after it.*), so the column is read-only there; today stays bookable all day. Tests: `MeetingRoomsView.test.tsx` (the picker, the lit slots, past days, 19:30 → 19:30–20:00), `lib/rooms/model.test.ts`, `book-form.test.ts`.

**REQUEST MANPOWER ASKS THE COMPANY'S OWN DEPARTMENTS (2026-10-12, mig `20261012220000`).** The user: Book a meeting room was linked to *Subsidiaries & departments*, Request manpower was not — "they use the same table". Its Department listed Hiring › Form settings' choices, which are HQ's (Revenue · Fair … Director Office): production's first guest request, HR-001 from Carres, was filed under *Finance*.
- **The page** (`lib/guest/model.ts` `companyDepartment`, `hireGroups(form, subs, co)`): step 2's Department lists the company picked in step 1, exactly as listed. Always the soft dropdown, never pills whatever its length (`isChips` leaves `dept` out, as it does the company) — as Book a meeting room's. A company with none listed gives a text box (*e.g. Operations*, 60 characters): Department is a core field, always required, so Book a meeting room's "asks none" would leave the guest unable to send. Picking another company empties it (`HireFlow` `set`). Form settings' Department choices stay HQ's — they are what *+ New request* asks.
- **The database** (`guest_link_hire`, production's body, one statement swapped by `pg_temp.swap_check`): a company with departments listed takes only one of them, as listed (*Pick a department from Carres's list — reload the page if it has changed.*); a typed one is 60 characters at most (*Keep the department under 60 characters.*); an empty one is still the Hiring module's to refuse (*Fill in Department.*). What a request keeps is unchanged: the answer is `answers.dept`, `requester_department` stays NULL (the guest's own department is not asked). `guest_link_page` is untouched. Hiring › Insights says whose a request is (`hiring.md` › INSIGHTS SAYS WHOSE REQUEST).
- **Tests:** `model.test.ts`, `GuestPage.test.tsx` (the company's list; another company empties it), PGlite `validate-guest-link.mjs` (its 220000 part: swapped once, still anon's, HQ's *Finance* refused, an exact match, typed at most 60, an empty one left to Hiring).
