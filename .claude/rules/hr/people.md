# HR tab — People

People Management (and its Permissions tab), Hierarchy (Org chart, Table, Levels, Roles), employee codes — `src/app/(portal)/team/`. How a permission template gates the portal is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 this was the Architecture tree's comment on `team/` in CLAUDE.md, moved word for word.

## `src/app/(portal)/team/`

team/ ← the sidebar's **People** section (its own category since 2026-09-01, below Departments): People Management / Member Services / Hierarchy. **Since 2026-10-08 (Claude Design handoff, the People Management revamp)** Permissions is People Management's 2nd tab (`?tab=permissions`: permission TEMPLATES — see Permissions below) and Roles is Hierarchy's 4th (`?tab=roles`: titles + Level + their template); /team/roles and /team/permissions redirect there. Invite user is a DRAWER on People Management (pick an active person with no login, email + password, no role); /team/people/invite? staff= deep-links into it. Routes stay under /team/*. Roles = titles. Hierarchy = the GRAPH, with a panel beside it (2026-09-07): three views on the header's rule (`?tab=`) — **Org chart** (default), **Table** (the same two facts as editable rows, in ladder order), **Levels** = the **ladder** (`title_levels`, one shared scale, 2026-09-03; on Roles until 2026-09-05) that the chart obeys, promotion climbs and the roster sorts by — `lib/ladder.ts`. A SECOND GRAPH rides the chart view: the commission tree (`comm_hierarchy`), READ beside the staff one and still EDITED on Revenue. Clicking a card in either centres the other. Permissions = the matrix, split off 2026-09-03. **Employee codes (2026-09-12):** `employee_code` = HV001, HV002 … numbered from the top of the ladder (CEO = HV001) and, from then on, handed out by trigger `hr_staff_costs_assign_code` = the largest HV number on the table + 1 (inactive rows count; a code is never cleared). Add Staff has no code field — it shows `next_staff_code()`; it DOES ask for a **Sub-department** and a **Reporting upline** (2026-09-20) — blank for a department with none, and a base whose ladder has no rung for the chosen title is offered greyed out. Inside one title the roster sorts by code (`compareByLadder`). Mig `20260915040000`. InfoTech's own number is `infotech_code` (mig `20260915050000`): both imports match on it first, name second, never on the HV code (`lib/import/staff-match.ts`).

**HIERARCHY IN THE REVAMP (2026-10-10, HR revamp 6/8; DESIGN.md v3.26; the user's A):** the kit head and three tabs — **Org chart** (the canvas, unchanged: the user kept it) · **People & roles** (count = titles) · **Levels**; `?tab=roles` and the old `?tab=table` both open People & roles, so every old link (Add staff, Permissions' title chips, `/team/roles`) still lands. **People & roles** (`_components/PeopleRoles`) replaces Table AND Roles: a band per title, most senior first (no rung last) — name, "L7 · 5 people" (· inactive, · heads its department), *Edit*, *Template: X* (→ People › Permissions on that template) — over the people holding it: Name, Reports to, Department, Permission template. **A PERSON'S PAGE EDITS WHERE IT STANDS (2026-10-14, the user, circling *Edit* on a person's full page: "when i press edit, no need jump to another tab, it should be can edit key in column"; DESIGN.md v4.10):** *Edit* no longer opens `/team/people/[id]/edit`. The page itself (`_components/PersonRecord`) turns each row's value into its field in the same section — Title, Employment Type and the statutory categories the soft dropdown, the name in the title's place; Department, Account and Role read as before — and the kit's peach save bar sticks to the sheet's foot (*Editing · N changes* · *Cancel* · *Save changes*, off until something changed; a changed field peach; a refusal in red until the next edit). **Employment Type is ONE select** where the old form had a tick in a commission department (Fully commission based) or the Director department (Variable pay) and a select elsewhere; it still sends exactly what `updateStaff` reads (`lib/person-record.ts` `payModeFields` — the ticks or `employment_type`), and the person's current basis is always among its choices, so a save that leaves the row cannot change it. The fields start from `getPersonCard` — its `can.edit` (Team · Edit, the Salary tick, the roster's own read) is who gets *Edit*; a masked NRIC or account shows in a box with no field and is not sent. `updateStaff` and `setEmployment` name `/team/people/[id]` as their page now — their buttons are there, and the same box decides (`hr.people` Edit; Permissions' and a department's People tabs open no `team.edit`) — and the save answers `{ ok, reason }` (production hides a thrown message). A change is counted against the value the browser drew: an address stored with `\r\n` (ANTHONY's, from the import) or an email with a stray space is not a change (`changedFields`). The old address still answers whoever it let in before, with `?edit=1`; `EditStaffForm` is gone (`StaffRow` moved to `lib/staff-form.ts`). No example values in empty fields — on a record a grey `850101141234` read as an NRIC on file.

**A SAVE SENDS ONLY WHAT CHANGED (2026-10-14, a side note the user passed on, the same day):** the old Edit page — and this page's first cut — sent the WHOLE record, every field as the page had loaded it; whoever saved last put back the older values over anything changed meanwhile (another HR, or the same shared login on another computer: a bank account changed at 10 went back to the old one when a phone was saved at 11). Now the page sends only the changed fields (`changedFormData`: a bank account as its bank and number together, a tick as `on` or blank, a new pay basis as its own fields) and `updateStaff` writes a column only when its field was sent — a name, a date, a contact or a pay figure it did not carry is never written (`payPatchForUpdate` replaced `payFieldsForUpdate`). Account 2 sent alone is checked against Account 1 on file (`readStaffPrivate`); an amount sent for a record somebody moved to commission or variable pay since the page opened is refused ("Their pay basis changed since this page was opened — reload it…"). Proved in two tabs on production data: B's contact survived A's later save. After a save the bar says *Saving…* until the server's fresh record is in (it lands ~2 s after the answer in dev), so the page never reads its old values back in that moment; 6 s without it, the page reads anyway.

**BASIC FROM COMMISSION (2026-10-14, the user, circling a Revenue person's Salary — *Fully commission based · None — 100% of pay is commission* — beside the paycheck's Basic salary RM 7,000: "他是从他的薪水扣，但他有给 basic 去扣 … 要可以勾选一个选项，设定成'他要给 basic，然后从他薪水扣'。只要勾选之后，在那边就可以填他的 basic 是多少"; DESIGN.md v4.15):** in a commission department (`payroll_source = 'commission'`) the person's page has no Employment Type select: the Salary section's *Pay basis* row is ONE tick, **Basic from commission** (`choosesByTick`). Off = Fully commission based (`commission_eligible`, basic 0); on = `salaried` with the basic typed (above 0 — a basic of 0 is Fully commission based), the two allowances and the Statutory section, since EPF / SOCSO / EIS / PCB are worked on the basic. Nothing new is stored: it is how KRIS and PETER (RM 12,000) already were, and what the payroll already does — a Revenue row's basic is paid OUT OF the month's commission (`lib/revenue-payroll.ts`, *Basic and commission are separate salary payments*), and Comm Cal's close and Create Period seed each new row's basic from the People record (no trigger carries a People change into a row already made — the month's row is keyed on Payroll). It reads *Basic from commission* (`payModeLabel`) on Employment Type, the pay basis and the person window. September 2026 had three Revenue bases typed on the grid only: CHEA HUAN 7,000, STANLEY 7,000, PEI FEN 2,500 (their records still Fully commission based at the time).
