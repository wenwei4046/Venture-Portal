# HR tab — People

People Management (and its Permissions tab), Hierarchy (Org chart, Table, Levels, Roles), employee codes — `src/app/(portal)/team/`. How a permission template gates the portal is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 this was the Architecture tree's comment on `team/` in CLAUDE.md, moved word for word.

## `src/app/(portal)/team/`

team/ ← the sidebar's **People** section (its own category since 2026-09-01, below Departments): People Management / Member Services / Hierarchy. **Since 2026-10-08 (Claude Design handoff, the People Management revamp)** Permissions is People Management's 2nd tab (`?tab=permissions`: permission TEMPLATES — see Permissions below) and Roles is Hierarchy's 4th (`?tab=roles`: titles + Level + their template); /team/roles and /team/permissions redirect there. Invite user is a DRAWER on People Management (pick an active person with no login, email + password, no role); /team/people/invite? staff= deep-links into it. Routes stay under /team/*. Roles = titles. Hierarchy = the GRAPH, with a panel beside it (2026-09-07): three views on the header's rule (`?tab=`) — **Org chart** (default), **Table** (the same two facts as editable rows, in ladder order), **Levels** = the **ladder** (`title_levels`, one shared scale, 2026-09-03; on Roles until 2026-09-05) that the chart obeys, promotion climbs and the roster sorts by — `lib/ladder.ts`. A SECOND GRAPH rides the chart view: the commission tree (`comm_hierarchy`), READ beside the staff one and still EDITED on Revenue. Clicking a card in either centres the other. Permissions = the matrix, split off 2026-09-03. **Employee codes (2026-09-12):** `employee_code` = HV001, HV002 … numbered from the top of the ladder (CEO = HV001) and, from then on, handed out by trigger `hr_staff_costs_assign_code` = the largest HV number on the table + 1 (inactive rows count; a code is never cleared). Add Staff has no code field — it shows `next_staff_code()`; it DOES ask for a **Sub-department** and a **Reporting upline** (2026-09-20) — blank for a department with none, and a base whose ladder has no rung for the chosen title is offered greyed out. Inside one title the roster sorts by code (`compareByLadder`). Mig `20260915040000`. InfoTech's own number is `infotech_code` (mig `20260915050000`): both imports match on it first, name second, never on the HV code (`lib/import/staff-match.ts`).

**PERMISSIONS IN THE REVAMP (2026-10-11, HR revamp 8a; DESIGN.md v3.29):** People › Permissions is the handoff's: "Title template" (a select of the templates, their count), Delete (asks; a template a title points at says to move them first) and "+ New template" (a copy of the one open until 2026-10-11; since then a window — name, views it can see, sensitive fields, a blank template with View on every tab of those views: `../portal-wide/permissions.md` › EVERY TAB OF EVERY VIEW), "Everyone holding X gets these…", then the matrix (`team/_components/TabMatrix`: the five views with "seen/of" counts, the open view's tabs × View · Create · Edit · Approve + all/none; a box a tab does not have is faint; since 2026-10-11 a Select all row on top for whoever may edit, and a ticked tab whose page would not open says why under its name), Save at the foot. Kept below the drawing: Sensitive fields, Titles on this template / with no template (the template select per title), History. The person window's Permissions is the same matrix, compact, from their title's ticks with a red dot on a box changed for them, and Reset to title · Save in the window's footer (`footSlot`, a portal); the full page draws them under it. Moving a title to another template from the titles table keeps its holders' own boxes over the new one (`permission_title_move`); a person whose boxes were saved over another template is told so in their window, Save enabled. The rules: `.claude/rules/portal-wide/permissions.md` › PERMISSIONS BY VIEW AND TAB.

**HIERARCHY IN THE REVAMP (2026-10-10, HR revamp 6/8; DESIGN.md v3.26; the user's A):** the kit head and three tabs — **Org chart** (the canvas, unchanged: the user kept it) · **People & roles** (count = titles) · **Levels**; `?tab=roles` and the old `?tab=table` both open People & roles, so every old link (Add staff, Permissions' title chips, `/team/roles`) still lands. **People & roles** (`_components/PeopleRoles`) replaces Table AND Roles: a band per title, most senior first (no rung last) — name, "L7 · 5 people" (· inactive, · heads its department), *Edit*, *Template: X* (→ People › Permissions on that template) — over the people holding it: Name, Reports to, Department, Permission template. Editing a title moved from Roles' inline rows into its **window** (`TitleWindow`, 420px of content): Name, Department, **Level** (a title's rung can now be set here as well as on Levels), Active (an existing title), Heads its department, the template as a link; Delete only while nobody holds it and only for whoever manages permissions; *+ New title* is in the head while People & roles is open. **Levels** (`_components/LevelsList`) is the handoff's: a row per rung, top first — "L7", its titles as chips in the handoff's tones (Team Leader purple, Team Assistant red, Senior Revenue Specialist orange, Revenue Specialist beige, every other title blue; `lib/hierarchy-roles.ts`), "No title on this level" on a cream row — kept editable: DRAG a chip to another row (or press it: *Move to L… / Take off the ladder*), click a level's NAME (right of the row) to rename it, *+ Add level* puts an empty one on top, an empty level shows *Remove*; titles on no rung sit in a last "Not on the ladder" row. Optimistic: a moved chip shows at once and goes back with the refusal (23DIR in its own words). `title-actions` / `ladder-actions` answer `{ ok, reason }` now (a thrown message reaches production's browser only as a digest). LevelsBoard, TitlesSection and StaffTable are gone.
