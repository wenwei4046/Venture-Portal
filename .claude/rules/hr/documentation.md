# HR tab — Documentation

People › Documentation, `/team/documentation` — `src/app/(portal)/team/documentation/`, `src/lib/documentation/`. DESIGN.md › "Documentation" is the visual spec; how a page and its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

**DOCUMENTATION (2026-10-11, the HR revamp's second package — README §10 of `.design/hr-r2/handoff`; migs `20261011070000`, `20261011070100`).** Four tabs, no counts on them: **Documents** · **Data center** · **Letters** · **Publish**; `?tab=documents|data-center|letters|publish` and `?person=<staff id>` open one. Function `documentation` (key `documentation`, tab `hr.documentation`): View reads the page and opens files, Create uploads and files and publishes, Edit deletes a document, withdraws a letter or a published memo, sets a letter type Compulsory, deletes a type. The user's answers (2026-10-11): everyone a memo or letter reaches must **Acknowledge** it — HR, Finance and the rest too, once everyone has their own login and the app; HR fills the missing Offer letters itself.

- **Documents** — a file shared with whole DEPARTMENTS (`hr_documents.department_ids`, portal departments). Everyone in them sees it in the Revenue app's Documentation (`member_documents()`, newest first, "new" until they open it — `member_document_open(id)` marks it seen and writes "Opened in Revenue app" on the trail). Delete is SOFT (`deleted_at`; asks once more, "Confirm delete"): it leaves the app at once and stays here under *Show deleted (n)* with its trail. The name defaults to the file's (`nameFromFile`). Refs `L-101`… (`hr_document_ref_seq`).
- **Data center** — every active person, a red "!" while a COMPULSORY letter type has nothing on their record. Upload (the 460px window: a letter type, or "+ Add a new letter type…" which names one; the file) or a missing row's own Upload files a letter to that person (`hr_letter_file`). **A filed letter is SENT**: it travels as a one-person memo (`member_memos.origin = 'data_center'`, `member_memo_recipients`), so its status is that memo's receipt — Delivered → Opened → Acknowledged — and its trail reads Uploaded · Sent to Revenue app (System) · Opened in Revenue app · Acknowledged (the person's own steps come from the receipt, `letterTrail`). Refs `D-101`… (`hr_letter_ref_seq`).
- **Letters** — every letter on file, of one type or all. The "Letter type" switcher holds the types: count, **Compulsory** (Edit; "Compulsory · N people missing" when one is picked), × (refused while letters of it are on file — *X has letters on file, so it cannot be deleted*), and a new type at its foot (it stays open on the new one, as the handoff's does). Seeds: Offer letter (compulsory), Increment letter, Warning letter. A name opens their record in Data center.
- **Publish** — REPLACES Member services › Publish memo (removed in the same PR; Member services keeps Request reviews · Brand weeks · Claim rates). A Memo, a Letter (and its letter type) or a type of HR's own (the Type switcher: added types have ×, Memo and Letter never; it closes after an add, as the handoff's does); Title, Body, an attachment; departments, each opening its people all ticked — untick who it should not reach. Refusals in the handoff's order: *Add a title.* · *Tick at least one person.* · *Add a body or attach a file.* · *Pick at least one department.* With a file, Publish goes outlined and **Publish & save** also files the attachment into every recipient's Data center (letters under the letter type, a memo under its own name). Sent from People & Culture, in force today, version 1, `requires_ack` — `hr_publish(...)`. The Published list shows a Letter under its letter type (mig `20261011070100`), the audience ("Revenue · everyone  /  HR · Grace Lim"), the file line and "N of M acknowledged".
- **The Revenue app is untouched.** A published memo has NO sub-department audience; it reaches exactly its recipients through two statements swapped into production's bodies: `member_visible_memo` also answers for `member_memo_recipients`, and `member_data_snapshot` sends `[]` (not null) for a memo with no sub-department. The Principal's View as lists them for the member being viewed (mig `20261011110100`: `member_principal_snapshot` names each memo's `recipients`; the app shows one only while viewing a recipient).
- **Files** live in the PRIVATE bucket `hr-documents` (25 MB; PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, pictures) under `documents/`, `letters/<staff id>/`, `publish/`. They go up from the BROWSER (`lib/documentation/upload.ts` — a handbook is bigger than a Server Action carries), the storage policy asking Documentation · Create; the Server Action that files it asks the database whether the object really arrived (`hr_doc_file_present`). They are read by a 300-second signed link made AT THE CLICK (`documentationFileLink` / `openMemberDocument`), never kept in a page's data; a member reads only a document of their department or a letter / attachment addressed to them (`hr_file_readable_by_member`). Opening writes Viewed, a download Downloaded.
- **The database.** Tables `hr_documents`, `hr_document_events`, `hr_document_seen`, `hr_letter_types`, `hr_publish_types`, `hr_person_letters`, `hr_letter_events`, `member_memo_recipients` (+ `member_memos` columns kind, letter type, attachment, origin, departments, audience label, saved): RLS on, NO policy — every read and write is a DEFINER function asking `has_any_action` for its cell (a member's, their own staff record). Readers `hr_documentation()`, `hr_documentation_file(kind, id)`, `member_documents()` are on `READS_WHILE_VIEWING_AS`; helpers `hr_doc_*` are the service role's only. Every Server Action answers `{ ok, reason }` (production masks a thrown message).
- **Code.** Parsing, wording and the arithmetic: `lib/documentation/model.ts` (tested); the page's reader and a file's link: `server.ts`; a member's: `member.ts` (for the Revenue app's coming Documentation screen: `loadMemberDocuments()`, `openMemberDocument(id, mode)`); the writes: `actions.ts`.

- **Excel and CSV preview (2026-10-11, the user forwarded the gap: the handoff's View draws a price list as a sheet).** The preview drawer draws an .xlsx / .xls / .csv file as the handoff's sheet (`previewKind` → `sheet`): the file's short link is fetched in the BROWSER and read by the portal's own SheetJS (`xlsx`, already a dependency for imports and exports) — `lib/documentation/sheet.ts`, run ONLY inside its own Web Worker (`sheet.worker.ts`, started by `read-sheet.ts` when a sheet is opened, ended with the answer, after 20 s, or when the drawer closes), because the file is somebody else's upload and npm's SheetJS 0.18.5 has known crafted-file flaws (prototype pollution, a slow regex): in the worker they can neither touch the page nor freeze it (`sheet.test.ts` fails a second importer; `sheet-view.ts` holds what the page draws without it) — the first sheet, as displayed, at most 200 rows × 26 columns (it says so when cut). - **Files really go, and a letter can be withdrawn (2026-10-11, the user's answer E after round 2's questions; mig `20261011170000`).** The user: a deleted file is deleted, and a letter sent to the wrong person can be taken back (PDPA) — only Documentation · **Edit** does either.
  - **Delete a document** stays soft for its ROW (greyed under *Show deleted*, its trail kept, out of the Revenue app at once) — and its FILE goes from the bucket.
  - **Withdraw** (Data center, on each letter of the record: *Withdraw* → *Confirm withdraw*, the armed pink of Delete; on a phone the three buttons take a line under the letter): `hr_letter_withdraw(id)`. Its person stops receiving it — they come off the memo it travelled as and their receipt goes; a memo that then reaches nobody goes too (a Data center letter's always does; a *Publish & save* memo keeps its other recipients, and its attachment stays theirs — and since mig `20261011200000` one that reaches nobody stays on the Published list as withdrawn). What the person had opened / acknowledged is written into the letter's trail first ("Opened in Revenue app", "Acknowledged"), then "Withdrawn — No longer on X's record or in the Revenue app". The letter STAYS on file as Withdrawn (`hr_person_letters.withdrawn_at` / `withdrawn_by`) and counts for NOTHING: not in a type's count, not on the record, not in the HR app's Data center (`liveLetters`), so a compulsory type flags its person with "!" again; a type whose letters were all withdrawn may be deleted. Withdrawn letters wait greyed under *Show withdrawn (n)* in Data center and Letters (Letters keeps the handoff's columns: status *Withdrawn*, View only). The preview of a deleted document or a withdrawn letter opens on its Audit trail and never asks for the file; `hr_documentation_file` signs no link to either; `hr_file_readable_by_member` no longer opens a withdrawn letter's file.
  - **The bucket.** The database refuses a direct DELETE on `storage.objects` (Supabase's `protect_delete`), so a file goes through the Storage API under the user's own session. The delete policy `hr_documents_delete` lets Documentation · Edit remove only a file NO live record holds (`hr_doc_file_unused`: no live document, no letter not withdrawn, no memo's attachment). After every delete and withdraw the Server Action removes what `hr_documentation_leftovers()` lists — deleted documents' and withdrawn letters' files, and an upload nobody saved once it is a day old (a Save that failed after its file went up), at most 100 — then `hr_documentation_files_removed(paths)` writes "File deleted" on each trail whose file the bucket no longer has (asked of the bucket, once). A file that would not go is listed again at the next delete or withdraw; it never holds up the answer.
  - **The rest is unchanged:** a withdrawn letter's row, trail and the audit log stay (HR's record that it was filed and withdrawn); nothing is deleted automatically by age except an unsaved upload.
