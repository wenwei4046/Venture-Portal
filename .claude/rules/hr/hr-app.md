# HR tab — the HR phone app (`/hr-app`)

The HR phone app — `src/app/(hr-app)/hr-app/`, `src/components/hr-app/`, `src/lib/hr-app/`. DESIGN.md › "The HR phone app" is the visual spec; what each part reads and writes is its module's own rules: Hiring (`hiring.md`), Documentation (`documentation.md`), Meeting Rooms (`rooms.md`), Member services (`../revenue/revenue-app.md`); how a page and its writes are gated is `../portal-wide/permissions.md`; installing it, `../portal-wide/pwa.md`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md.

**THE HR APP (2026-10-11, the HR revamp's second package — README §17, `HR Mobile App.dc.html`, the canvas's artboard 4a; the user: "HR app at /hr-app, installable", only for accounts with HR's view; DESIGN.md v3.38).** Its own address and route group, `(hr-app)/hr-app` — NOT a portal page: no sidebar, no `requirePage`, its own layout (the warm ground `#f6f2ec`, the theme colour, `appleWebApp` title *HR*) and one client screen, `components/hr-app/HrApp.tsx`, on `data-hr-app` (the Revenue app's floor rules in globals.css cover it too: browser-default controls, no portal `h1`). Five tabs — **Home · Review · Hiring · Rooms · More** — and pages pushed over them (a request, a member request, Insights, the Talent Bank, a talent, the Data center, a person), sheets rising from the bottom (`mSheet .28s`), a pinned tab bar standing on `--safe-bottom`.
- **Who opens it:** an account holding one of HR's own areas — Hiring, Documentation or Member services' requests (`opensHrApp`, the keys `hiring` / `documentation` / `member_requests_manage`); anybody else goes to the portal. Signed out, it sends you to `/login?next=/hr-app` — the ONE return the sign-in honours (`RETURNS` in `(auth)/login/page.tsx`; any other `next` is ignored, so the address can never send a signed-in person off the portal) — so the installed app opens on itself again.
- **What it reads** (`lib/hr-app/server.ts` `loadHrAppData`, every read under the viewer's own session, side by side): Member services' requests (`adminMemberAction({ action: 'list' })` + `member_admin_request_facts`), the meeting rooms of the seven days from today (`readRoomsRange`, `readRoomsPeople`), People › Documentation's Data center (`loadDocumentation` → everybody, the letter types, the letters on file) and Hiring (`loadHiringBoard` — requests with candidates and trails, the sources, the Talent Bank, HR's owners). Each asks its own cell first (`canDo` / `can`), and a part the viewer may not read is simply empty — the app still draws. A member request shows by the number HR and the member both quote (`RQ-…`), a hiring request by its `HR-…`; dates are the portal's three-letter months (`formatIntlDate` — en-GB writes September "Sept"); the greeting goes by the office clock (`officeTime`).
- **What it does** (`components/hr-app/HrAppLive.tsx`): every button is the module's OWN Server Action, which asks its own cell and the database again — Hiring's Acknowledge (pick the owner among HR), Request more info, Decline, Cancel, Mark filled (one hired candidate, the source, the cost), a candidate's stage, New candidate (name, phone, a note — shortlisted, as *Applied*), a talent's notes and Remove (`lib/hiring/actions.ts`); Review's member requests — **Accept** onto the waiting list (`qualify`), a claim's **Approve** into the payroll (`route: 'payroll'`, the desktop's main button), **Reject** (`adminMemberAction`); Book a room (`bookRoom`, `source: 'hr_app'`, from a department) and Cancel meeting (only whoever booked it; a guest-link booking with Meeting rooms · Edit — `cancelBooking`); a letter's **View** (`openDocumentationFile('letter', …)`, a short link made at the press and opened in a tab readied in the tap). After a write the app reads again (`router.refresh()`).
- **Look:** every value is the handoff's (`hr-app.module.css`), measured against the canvas element by element (`.design/hr-r2/tools/states-hrapp.json`). Its selects are the kit's soft dropdown WITH the soft field — the canvas's global `select{}` rule (radius 10, the chevron, `!important`) draws the phone apps' fields too. Not in the handoff (its sample always has them): *No meeting rooms yet.* on Rooms, *No meetings today.* under Home's heading, and — in the Review's own *Nothing waiting.* card — *No requests here.* on Hiring and *Nobody kept on file yet.* in the Talent Bank.
- **Installing:** none of its own since 2026-10-11 — it is HR's view of the ONE app (ONE APP, below). Until then it had `public/hr-app.webmanifest` (*HOUZS HR*), swapped in by `HrAppManifest`; both are deleted.
- **Preview:** `/hr-app/zz-preview` draws the app on the handoff's own sample — git-excluded (`.git/info/exclude`), local only.

**THE APP FOLLOWS THE ACCOUNT'S TABS, PAGE BY PAGE (2026-10-11, the round's security review, M2).** `/hr-app` is no portal page, so a write sent from it named no page in its Referer and the modules' gates acted "as held" (`actingUser`, `lib/auth.ts`), and the reads ignored the ticks. Now each part of the app IS a portal page (`PART_PAGE`, `lib/hr-app/server.ts`): Review's member requests `/member-admin`, Hiring `/hiring/requests`, Insights `/hiring/insights`, the Talent Bank `/hiring/talent`, the Data center `/team/documentation`, Rooms `/admin/rooms`. `appAccess(user)` answers `parts` — a part shows only while its cell AND its page's tab are open (Requests closed but Insights open: no request is listed or reviewed, Insights still counts them) — and `may`, the writes behind the buttons: the account narrowed to that page's open boxes (`actionsOnPage`) before `canDo` asks, so a box unticked on one page closes its write there only (Talent Bank · Edit unticked: no notes, no Remove, while Requests' Edit still moves candidates). A button they may not use is not drawn; the slots of a room take no tap. Every write goes through `lib/hr-app/actions.ts`, which asks the same `appAccess(...).may` first and answers *This is not open to your account.* before the module is ever called (`actions.test.ts`) — the module's own gate and the database still ask after it.

**THE PHONE PARITY PASS (2026-10-11, the second audit of the three phone surfaces — `.design/hr-r2/verify-phone/REPORT.md` — and the roles walkthrough).**
- **A sheet's field keeps its place while it is typed in.** `Field` / `SelectField` were components made afresh inside `SheetView` on every render, so React swapped each input for a new one after every key — *Interview* left *I*, the people search kept *N*. They live at module scope now (`HrApp.test.tsx` types key by key and asks for the same element, still focused). **Never declare a component inside another's body.**
- **Attending and Total pax are groups (`div`), not labels:** a label clicks its first button when its text is pressed — pressing *Attending* removed the first person, *Total pax* took one off (the prototype's × and − are spans, so its labels never did). The people search is named *Add a colleague*.
- **As the canvas:** selects `border-box` (46px, not 48); a sheet at most `calc((100dvh − the top inset − --safe-bottom) × .86 + --safe-bottom)` — the prototype's `calc(86% + 30px)` of the screen between the bars (its top 160 on a 390×844 phone, not 118); the body's slide-in plays once (the key that remakes a screen sits on a Fragment inside it, not on the animated `.body`); HR notes keep the browser's 2px; every field's text the browser's black (`color` on each field class — Tailwind's preflight makes a `:where()` rule lose); no grey tap flash. The grab bar stays: the README's screenshot draws it (the canvas runtime draws it 0px tall, so there the title sits 5px higher).
- **A claim's Approve asks where the money goes (the user, 2026-10-11 — "两个都给"):** *Approve claim*, a sheet with **Request payment** and **Add to payroll** — Member services' two routes (`hrAppDecide(id, 'approve', note, route)`, `route` `payroll` | `payment`, anything else refused). A waiting list's *Accept to waiting list* still sends at once.
- **A press on the timeline's last half hour** books 19:30–20:00 (`hourFrom`, `lib/hr-app/model.ts`), not 19:30–20:30, which showed *Choose…* and was refused.
- **Viewing as a person** (the roles audit, P2): the app greets the person viewed, not the signed-in account the database answers for, names nobody as an attendee, and carries the portal's own read-only strip (`ViewingAsBanner` — *Viewing as X … Read-only: nothing you press is saved.* / Stop viewing). Writes were already refused.
- **Small ones from the same audit:** a member request's page names its status in the Revenue app's words (`requestStatusLabel` — it read the raw `needs_info`); `+ Book` greys out while there is no room yet (in the Revenue app too), as the portal's *+ Book a room*; Insights' *Days to fill* says *No request filled yet.* instead of an empty card.
