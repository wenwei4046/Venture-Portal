# HR tab — HR Costing

The unified Payroll (staff, the Revenue team and the Directors on one grid; Complete, batches to Finance, Resubmit / Second payroll, Total Close), the Payment statement, Billing Allocation and department mode — `src/app/(portal)/hr-costing/`.

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (a review, QA, an E2E audit) — CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab. New decisions are added here, dated, never to CLAUDE.md. Until 2026-10-10 this was the Architecture tree's comment on `hr-costing/` in CLAUDE.md, moved word for word.

## `src/app/(portal)/hr-costing/`

hr-costing/ (Phase 3 — staff directory moved to team/) payment/ = monthly Payment statement (2026-09-11): shared reporting above Salary / Statutory tabs. Salary alone contains prior-month pay changes. Both use Generate by Department or Manual generate (select employees), with a reviewed snapshot before write. Salary coverage: payroll_voucher_lines; statutory: payroll_statutory_voucher_lines. SOCSO/EIS are separate schemes under PERKESO and generated atomically. Staff/director statutory sources stay in HR custody. Compiled statutory PVs allow date/notes edits only. payment_cycle includes exact coverage and completion; lib/payment-cycle.ts drives reporting. DESIGN.md: Payment statement (v3.7). payroll/ grid (2026-09-30): EVERY statutory figure is a field — EPF/SOCSO/EIS EE + PCB under Deductions, ER under Employer. A typed one lives in `*_override_amount` (the value column is never written by hand) and the compute trigger applies it over JADUAL on every recompute until Reset to JADUAL; the InfoTech import still writes its file's figures verbatim. The row's ▾ is gone (the name / ⋯ open the paycheck). `lib/payroll-grid.ts` payrollRowWrite, mig `20260930010000`.

**THE UNIFIED PAYROLL (2026-09-27):** payroll/ [period] is everybody's pay on one grid — staff keyed here, the Revenue team closed on Comm Cal, Directors Completed on Director Salary — and a person moves draft → completed → submitted → paid (enum value `completed`, mig 20260927060500). Tick → *Complete* (staff only; a salesperson completes when their `comm_salesperson_closes` row stands — trigger `comm_close_sync_payroll` sets the row's commission to Σ standing closes and records the close's bills in `comm_order_releases` under the month's run; a Director on `director_payroll_complete`, which still writes the billing snapshot). *Submit to Finance* = `payroll_submit_batch`: everybody Completed, as one BATCH (`payroll_batches` + a line per person in `payroll_batch_lines`), its own funding request and accrual JVs (reference = the batch) — pressable again for the next group (user: Finance 3 today, HR 5 next week); what each batch waits on is shown in *Submitted to Finance*, never on the button. The month keeps ONE payroll_run = Σ standing batches, so billing and dashboards read it as before. Right-click → *Resubmit* or *Second payroll* (`payroll_open_topup(scope, row, kind)`) unlocks somebody already sent — the same move, recorded apart (a correction vs a planned second payment, e.g. a sub-department paying on the 15th: `topup_kind` on the row and the batch line, `payroll_batches.second_payrolls`; mig `20260927070000`); amounts may only go UP and the next batch carries the difference — Finance funds the CTC delta, HR › Payment pays the net delta (`payroll_salary_entitlements`: submitted, FUNDED, covered, paid per component; statutory the same). *Total Close* (`payroll_close_month`) locks the month; only the Principal reopens (`payroll_reopen_month`). Finance sends back ONE batch (`payroll_send_back_batch`: request cancelled, JVs reversed, its people Completed again). Every status move goes through those functions (guard `guard_payroll_row_lifecycle`, `app.payroll_lifecycle`). Retired: the whole-month `submit_payroll_to_principal`, Director Salary's own Submit to Finance and Comm Cal's whole-month Send (they raise 23PAY). June–August are batch #1 (legacy) of their month. `lib/payroll-lifecycle.ts`, mig `20260927061000`, DESIGN.md › Payroll batches.

**BILLING ALLOCATION FOLLOWS THE PAYROLL (2026-09-30):** allocation/[period] is one card per payroll row of the month in a COST-CENTER department, in the grid's order (`comparePayrollOrder`); centralized departments are named in the header, not listed (`allocationRoster` — anybody who still HAS a split stays listed, so nothing billed is hidden). A person's split LOCKS with their row: Completed, with Finance, paid, or the month Closed for good. The way back is the payroll's own — Unlock, reopen on Comm Cal, or Resubmit / Second payroll (a draft again while open). Every split row belongs to its payroll row (FK `hr_staff_allocation_payroll_row_fkey`, ON DELETE CASCADE — removed with it) and trigger `trg_hr_staff_allocation_follows_payroll` refuses the rest (23PAY, same words as the page). Copy from last month fills only people on this month's payroll and still a draft. `splitLock` in lib/staff-allocation.ts, mig `20260930040000`.

**DEPARTMENT MODE (2026-09-30):** `departments.cost_model` = `cost_center` (only passes its cost through: split on Billing Allocation, pool should net to zero — HR, Finance, IT, Director Office) or `centralized` (bills the subsidiaries by INVOICE, runs its own pool, not on Billing Allocation — Director, Marketing, Revenue). An on/off switch (`components/department/DepartmentModeSwitch`, on = centralized) in Settings › Departments' Mode column and on each department's own page (HR Costing, Service Billing, Director Pool, Revenue › Department; Marketing once it has a view). Only permission `department_mode` (Principal by default) switches it, and it asks about the person SIGNED IN — the Principal flips it from HR's or Finance's view too — so the gate is `department_set_cost_model`'s `has_perm`, not `requirePermission`. Going centralized clears that department's splits on DRAFT payroll; the guard refuses a new split on a centralized department's person. NOT tied to `own_share_billback`: a cost center that did not net to zero must stay VISIBLE, never be absorbed by Venture automatically (user 2026-09-30). `lib/department-mode.ts`, mig `20260930050000`.

**NO DESCRIPTIONS ON THE PAGE (2026-10-12, the user on HR's pages: "find all this wording … like a description, i want delete it, cause this cause the view too messy"; DESIGN.md v3.75 › No descriptions on the page):** HR Costing, Director Salary and Director Pool lost their explanatory lines — the payroll grid's Revenue notice, toolbar hint and foot paragraph, the batches' foot, the import pages' subtitles and *imported verbatim* cards, Salary PVs' foot, Billing Allocation's *· unallocated share stays with HV* and the Not listed tail (now *Not listed: X (12) and Y (32) — centralized.*), Director Salary's section ledes, notices and table feet, Director Pool's settings / section notes and the Advance lede, and the department-mode switch's meaning line and *Switched by the Principal.* (the disabled switch's tooltip says *Only the Principal can switch this*; `costModelSummary` went). The payroll row menu is one line a row like the bill menu: *Resubmit…*, *Second payroll…*, *Reset statutory to JADUAL* and the disabled *Resubmit · Second payroll* say what they do or why not in their tooltips, the menu's head shows the status word with its detail on hover, and the *Shift + right-click…* hint went (Shift still opens the browser's menu). Kept: the live-status lines (*N directors waiting to go to Finance — …*, *To complete: …*), data warnings, empty states, the payslips (documents).

**HOUZS VENTURE'S OWN SHARE (2026-10-13, the user on Director Salary's Pool Breakdown: "i want add on houzs venture itself in this as well. but when press save, once detech venture itself got pay, will need to select the amount is deduct from which department. then can pick more than 2 department as well, by amount or percentage from the total amount. then confirm so that finance will get the submittion to make the inter transfer fund" — and "that fund can be select from all department pay. so make sure all department, can be seleceted"):** the Pool Breakdown opens on a **HOUZS Venture · HV** row: Basic / KPI / Allowance keyed as a subsidiary's, its employer statutory looked up from B+K at one head (`compute_director_ctc_total`, the ER override kept), counted in the Director Pool Total. HV is no company (`companies` holds the subsidiaries) and nobody invoices it: its row is `director_hv_shares` (one a month) and the departments that pay it `director_hv_share_lines` — EVERY department may, the Director department (its line moves nothing: the Director pool bears it) and Venture Office included, by amount or by a percentage of the total; the lines make the total to the sen, a percentage's rounding going to its biggest line (`lib/director-hv-share.ts` is the database's arithmetic, so the window shows what is saved). Save, while HV pays anything, first opens the departments window (`HvSplitWindow`); Confirm saves the grid with the split and puts the share *With Finance* (the row's chip); HV at zero saves with no window and clears the share. Once Finance has transferred it the row is locked (*Transferred*) until Finance's Undo — `director_hv_share_save` refuses a change (23PAY) and the grid sends nothing for it. The Submission tab lists the share (department, %, amount, invoice · receipt) and counts it in Billing vs Payroll's pool (*Subsidiaries + HOUZS Venture*). The tables are read and written only by their functions (RLS on, no client grants): `director_hv_share_of` (Director Salary · View), `director_hv_share_save` (· Edit); a refusal comes back as `{ ok: false, reason }` from `batchSaveCTC`.
