# HR tab — Attendance (Today, Records, Requests, Leave balances, Month close, Settings, Readiness)

Read this before changing anything it covers, and again in every full scan of such a change (CLAUDE.md › Rules by tab). New decisions are added here, dated.

**THE MODULE (2026-10-15, the Claude Design handoff "Attendance Prototype Design" — `design_handoff_hr_portal`, README + `source/HR Attendance.dc.html`; the user: "做出 100% 一样的页面 … help me link the backend as well"):** seven pages under `/attendance/*`, HR's sidebar section II (olive), one HR-view tab each (`hr.att_today` … `hr.att_ready`, `lib/permission-tabs.ts`, page registry reseeded by mig `20261015030300`). One function `attendance` (View · Create · Edit · Approve, reach all; key `attendance`, scope none; the Principal, HR and the Ultimate / Full / HR templates hold it — mig `20261015030000`).

**THE DATA (migs `20261015030100` tables, `030200` functions):** 17 `att_*` tables, RLS on and NO policy — every read and write is an `att_*` SECURITY DEFINER function asking `has_any_action(attendance.*)` (readers `att_config`, `att_month`, `att_requests_list`, `att_ledger`; writers listed in the migration's head). What a DAY is (P / L / A / I / leave / H / W / `·`) is never stored: `lib/attendance/engine.ts` reckons it from who clocks (Settings › Who clocks: person → title → company, latest `from` within each), the schedule, the holidays and whole-day overrides, approved / waiting leave, the punches and HR's corrections (`att_day_marks`, which win). Stored are the facts and HR's decisions. Balances: entitlement from the leave type's groups (first match by title + level, service steps by whole years from `hire_date`), AL-like types accrue monthly (`accrues`), the rest granted on 1 January; used = approved leave requests + leave codes set in Records; openings and adjustments are `att_ledger` rows with a reason. Attendance starts EMPTY — HR sets it up on Settings; the Act's OT minimums and a first policy (from 2026-11-01) are seeded. `att_months` freezes a month (the register the page reckoned); a closed month refuses corrections until reopened with a reason.

**PUNCHING (the user, 2026-10-15):** the punch button is the user's to add — in the Revenue app, the HR app, and later the Marketing and Finance apps. The portal only keeps the backend ready: `att_punch(direction, lat, lng, accuracy, device, photo_path, reason, source)` punches for the caller's own staff record (cutover → work day, nearest fence → inside / distance, refused outside only when the policy says Refuse). Do not build a punch UI unasked.

**REQUESTS:** a request takes its route when sent (title override → company row → the leave type's own); the upline is stored on the step then, and an upline without a login falls back to HR. HR (Attendance · Approve) decides HR's step, or the upline's on their behalf — recorded as such; the upline decides their own step signed in as themselves; nobody decides their own request; rejecting needs a note. Leave balances › Use leave books leave approved on the spot (`p_booked`).

**THE LOOK:** the design system's core components live in `components/ds` (Button, Field / TextField / Textarea / DsSelect, SegmentedControl, Switch, StatusChip, DescriptionList, DataTable, ListItem, Card, Banner, Sheet / SidePanel / CenterWindow / Toast), ported value for value from the handoff's `_ds_bundle.js`; page pieces in `attendance/_components/bits.tsx`. Compare with the standalone prototype at a **1600×1000** viewport — at 1440×900 its stage scales to 0.947. No page subtitle, no "drag / click here" hint lines (DESIGN.md › No descriptions on the page), though the prototype draws them.

**MONTH CLOSE SENDS HQ'S PAYROLL (2026-10-15 — asked whether a close should write the OT hours and the unpaid days into that month's payroll, the user: "要，自动写进去"; on InfoTech: "will no longer use infotech"; then "先只做 HQ", "跳过，列出来", and, shown the arithmetic on Ali — basic RM 2,600, 8-hour days, 6 h on a work day × 1.5 + 4 h on a rest day × 2 = RM 212.50, 2 unpaid days → basic × 29/31 — "对，照这样做"; DESIGN.md v4.51):**
- **The close is worked out on the server.** `closeMonth(month)` (`lib/attendance/actions.ts`) reads the facts itself — the page no longer hands in a register — works out what payroll is sent BEFORE freezing (a day type with no rate refuses the close), freezes the register (`att_close_month`, now with *OT hours* · *Rest-day OT hours* · *Holiday OT hours*), then sends payroll (`lib/attendance/payroll-write.ts` `sendToPayroll`). A failure after the freeze is said, never thrown: the month stays closed, the toast says payroll was not reached.
- **Who gets it:** each HQ person the register counts — not the Revenue team (a commission row or department; Comm Cal's), not a director (variable pay; Director Salary's). Only for a closer holding HR Costing · Edit on Payroll (`mayActOn`, lib/auth.ts), written under their own session, so the rows' policies, the payroll trigger and the lifecycle guard answer as for a save on HR › Payroll. A draft row takes `ot_hours`, `ot_amount`, `unpaid_leave_days` and `attendance_figures` (the priced lines, the dates, the close's `closedAt`); a person with no row gets the row Create period makes (active record, Create too); a row Completed, with Finance or paid is left and named. Reopen and Close again sends again — a later close's figures replace the earlier ones.
- **The price** (`lib/attendance/payroll.ts`, pure): ORP = the base ÷ 26 (Settings › OT rules' `otRates`; the base is the policy in force that day — basic, or basic + the row's statutory allowance), HRP = ORP ÷ a full day's hours — the person's schedule's longest work day, 8 without one — kept to 4 decimals; a line = hours × HRP × the day type's multiplier, to the sen. The day type is the request's (HR picks it), else what the day is (H → holiday, CH / SO → off, the schedule's rest / off). Who clocks › OT: none keeps the hours on the line, unpaid; "by wage" the same when the row's basic + statutory allowance is above the policy's line (THE USER'S ANSWERS, below). OT is priced on the row's basic at the close: a basic changed after it keeps the old OT until the month is closed again.
- **Unpaid days:** days on an unpaid leave type (the type's `unpaid` flag, not the code) and absent / incomplete days decided Unpaid — a decision on a day no longer absent or incomplete no longer counts, in the register too.
- **Approved OT of somebody the month does not count** (no Who clocks that month) is not sent — HR keys it on Payroll.
- **Outputs › Payroll**, for a reader of HR Costing whose Payroll tab is open (`payrollMarksFor`, its GUARDS line in `src/test/read-paths.ts`): *All N in payroll* / *N in payroll · M not sent*, each one not sent with its reason. "In" means the row carries THIS close's `closedAt`.

**THE USER'S ANSWERS (2026-10-15, evening — the questions left from the night's build; DESIGN.md v4.56, mig `20261015200000`):**
- **OT "by wage" follows the Act's wage line** — asked about Ben, basic RM 4,500, 10 hours on a work day (RM 324.52, or recorded and not paid?): "要，超过 RM4,000 不付". `att_policy.ot_wage_cap` (RM 4,000 to start; Settings › Policy › *By wage: OT paid up to*): a "by wage" line whose row's basic + statutory allowance is ABOVE it is priced at 0 with `unpaid: 'over_line'` — the hours stay on the register and in `attendance_figures`; at the line it pays; statutory always pays. A new Who clocks assignment opens on **by wage** ("set default by wage").
- **Leave type flags are switches** ("窗口加开关"): *Earned month by month* (`accrues`), *Unpaid*, *Comes out of* (`draws_from`), *Counts calendar days*. A new type's code fills them (AL earns, UL unpaid, EL out of AL, maternity / paternity calendar days — `derivedFlags`, `lib/attendance/settings.ts`) until HR turns one; saved, an unpaid type earns nothing and comes out of nothing (`settledFlags`). Payroll's unpaid days follow the flag, never the code.
- **No hire date** ("Readiness 提醒，到时再补"): Readiness names who clocks with none — their service steps never come. The 36 without one today are the Revenue team and the directors; nobody fills them yet.
- **Policy and Who clocks start on a month's 1st, the next at the earliest** ("对，从下个月 1 号").
- **A profile change, approved, writes the People record** ("批准后自动改"): `att_create_request` keeps only phone, email, address, the emergency contact (name, phone, relationship), bank and account number — each with what the record held — and refuses anything else, a field named twice, or nothing to change; a profile change never ends at the upline (Upline only becomes Upline → HR: "银行户口一定要 HR 批"); HR's last approval writes exactly those fields in one UPDATE (*Record updated* on the trail). An account number is •••• to a reader whose title does not tick bank accounts — in the database (`att_requests_list`) and for View as (`shownChanges`).
- **Uplines are People's `reports_to`** ("可以，用 reports_to"); they will approve in the apps, later ("以后在 app 里批") — until then HR decides on their behalf. Do not add an upline-only view to the portal page unasked.
- **Files** ("现在开好"): the private bucket `attendance` — a person uploads only into their own folder (`punch/<login>/…`, `request/<login>/…`); nobody overwrites or deletes; Attendance · View reads every file, a person their own. `att_punch` refuses a photo path that is not the caller's own filed object; `att_request_attach(request, path, name)` files an attachment on a request still waiting (its person, or Attendance · Create); the portal opens a file through `attendanceFileLink` — a ten-minute signed link made at the press (`att_file` names it; View as may read it). Requests › *Download* / *View* and the person calendar's IN / OUT tiles use it. **Not done:** deleting photos after Policy's *Photos kept* days — there is no sweep yet.
- **Remind all** ("现在就做"): Today › *Not in yet* ends in *Remind all* (Attendance · Edit) → `att_remind`: a notice (kind `attendance`, ref `att_today`) to each person with no valid IN today who has a login, not again within 10 minutes, pushed at once (`pushSoon`; a tap opens the app's front page, `/hr-app` or `/member`) and never listed in a bell (`notifications_mine` leaves the kind out). The toast says who could not get one (no login, or no phone signed up). The push needs `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` on the server.
- **Fixed hours on Today** ("只在请假时显示"): a No clock-in person shows only on a day they are on approved leave, in *On leave*.
- **Mark punch invalid** ("放在月历那一天"): under the picked day's IN / OUT tile in the person calendar, for Attendance · Edit — a reason, then *Mark punch invalid* (`att_invalidate_punch`); the day is worked out again without it, and an invalid punch is listed struck through with its reason.
